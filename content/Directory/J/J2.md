## Ripple Road: The Lown (1)  
Etto Homobonus    Teleport (10)    5/14/4/2    J2 (1)    weaver  
This hostel is always at capacity, or so it seems. Its guests stay for months at a time and regularly return when the world gets too much for them. Here at the Lown they find peace in its chintzy halls, deep sofas, large regular meals, and long and early sleep. Etto, the manager, a tidy man, well dressed and ready to serve you with firm politeness at any hour of the day, runs the place. His staff all believe in the importance of the project and the healing process the guests are experiencing and will go to any length to preserve the quietude.  
  
## Ripple Road: Ripplestone (2)   
Severinus Vulga    Thunder    4/15/3/3    J2 (2)    ball-maker  
  
Severinus, young and athletic, recently inhereted his fathers trade of making balls, coshes, and protective gear for the game of ripplestone. Ripplestone is a ball game whereby two teams squabble over possession of a leather ball and attempt to get it to the opposite end of the street. Traditionally it has been played along Ripple Road between Tallow Street and the Plaza of Time, each being the goal of the opposing teams. The game is overseen by the Alcalde but they are mainly concerned with keeping the gamblers from interfering and otherwise ignore all but the most egregious violence amongst the players. Severinus'fine ball is hurled from the balcony of Jaspar's Fine Mare (H4) to start the games but is usually reduced to several ragged chunks by the end of the day.  
  
## Behind Ripple Road: Factional's Geloscopticians (3)  
Dr Factional Parmenius    5/14/2/0    Earthquake (5)    SPARE        geloscopist  
  
Dr Parmenius is an elderly man whose bones are trying to escape his parchment skin, and his surgery is marked by healthful advice posters plastered on every surface. They must go back decades and warn of the dangers of everyday activities from napping before midday to eating pickled fish. Fortunately, in these nylon carpeted halls, one can be assessed for any ailment in one simple procedure. A nurse will bring you before Dr Parmenius and instruct you to laugh, from the belly if you will. If you have trouble they can provide reading material to induce jocularity. Once a deep laugh has been issued the doctor can judge your state of health and issue any medicines neccesery to bring you to full fitnss in short order. Come back in two weeks.
