## Owl Street: Notary Office Ursus (1)  
Eanfleda Affliction    Animate (2)    8/11/3/0    J6 (4)    notary  
A large office with strip lighting above a huge waiting room which always has someone sleeping in it, if not outright camping in some quieter corner. Ticket numbers pass by on the flapper board with no one taking them. One might think one could just enter an appointment with the Congress' notaries, since no one is standing up to take the spot, but no ticket no meeting. You must pull out, one by one, months worth of tickets until you catch up with the current number, only then can you be seen. Eanfleda is the youngest notary working here and she has been here for longer than most people have been alive. She suspects the congress has forgotten they exist but their pay packets still come in regularly, and the money they still receive for deceased staff pays for enough maintenance to keep the rain out, so it's not so bad.

Wise Laszlo    Climb    6/17/2/0    J6 (3)    stonecutter  
  
Wise Laszlo came to fame for scaling the face of Typhon Megarex, the highest known mountain/statue in the city. The month long vertical ascent was thought to be impossible, so much so that no one bothered to meet him at the crown, assuming him to be engaged in a roundabout suicide. When he clambered over the rim and collapsed in front of a tour group word spread quickly, so when he awoke an impromptu party was in full swing above the clouds and his celebrity was sealed. Unable to climb any more he spent the small fortune he amassed through party appearances and dinner speeches on a boarding house cum Wise Laszlo museum. While his general celebrity has cooled the long term residents of The Up And Down are all megafans who ensure the decrepit star feels that time hasn't moved on.
