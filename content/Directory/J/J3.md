# J3  
## Tallow Street: The Rhubarb and Custard (1)  
Almachius Twinkle    7/16/2/1    Teleport (10)    SPARE        innkeeper  
  
The floors, walls and ceiling are tiled, white with dust stained grease. Tables and chairs, none matching, are crammed into the small empty cafe. To get to the counter one must climb over or crawl under the furniture, Almachius will not accept shouted orders. He is a here seemingly all hours, grilling something or other, wiping his hands on his filthy blue and white striped apron or through his thin slick hair. Customers are rare, and they always position themselves as far from each other as possible.  
  
## Tallow Street: The Sword Show (2)  
Gus Creep    6/14/4/1    Drown (4)    SPARE        embroiderer  
Restituta Flare    5/9/2/0    Assume Shape    SPARE        Leather dresser  
  
A blacked out building, boarded windows and beaded doors. Shows open twice a day, after lunch and dinner. 20p is taken on the door by a pole thin youth, but never the same twice. Gus, a barrel bellied man wrapped in crab fisherman's clothes, seats the audience and introduces the act: Restituta Flare. Audience memebers are invited one by one to come up and stick a sword, provided by the impassive Gus, in the supine body of Restituta. Sign of discomfort seems forced, and with little enthusiasm behind her eyes. Two shows a day, every day.  
  
## Behind Tallow Street: Youthful Attraction (3)  
Youthful Trason    5/10/2/0    Thought Vapour (1)    SPARE        Blacksmiths  
  
Trason comes from the other side of town. His ways are strange, he looks confusing, and speaks with a rolling accent, and the kids love him. Gamins come here to receive his piercings, hand made and hand riveted. They stick their little heads on his anvil and he expertly applies the tunnels and bolts. The gangs take them as a point of pride, knowing that to give your opponent additional hand holds is reckless. Anyone messing with Trason will be visited by 10d6 armed gamins within the week.  
  
## Tallow Street: The Trough  
Ursus Trough    5/11/2/1    Protection from Rain (1)    SPARE        scale maker  
  
This hotel is very rarely staffed, the guests work themselves out. A sign with the rates sits on the floor by the empty reception booth and you are expected to put the money behind the counter and lean across to grab a key. Screams, crashes, brutal stormings out, the place is noisey and dirty, teeming with life of all sorts at all hours. 10p per night, leave the key on the way out, and if you don't pay expect a visit from Mr. Trough's toughs.
