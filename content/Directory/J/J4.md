## Blue Lane: Tudy Towers (1)  
Idle Tudy    Flash (3)    5/10/2/3 (commonwealth plasma proof clothes)    J4 (1)    Furniture Makers  
  
Blue Lane was constructed to relieve the conjestion of Ripple Road, by order of a sign put up in the name of the universal congress. Construction started straight away and the ancestral home of the Tudy's was partially demolished. It turned out tha tthe sign was a forgery and not from the Congress at all, rather it was a well constructed ruse by one of Tudy's business rivals, placed in vengeace of some now forgotten slight. Without instructions to the contrary the road was never removed and the Tudy's instead turned their fractured mansion into a series of irregularly shaped turrets connected by covered walkways. Idle Tudy, marked by their distinctive familial concave head, is the latest in a long line of ineffectual Tudy heirs whose only contribution to the name is not dying before having an heir.  
  
## Tallow Street: Landaulette Cards (2)  
Gregory Coil    Assassin’s Dagger (2)    8/6/2/0    J4 (2)    Makers of Playing Cards  
  
Every house has a pack of Landaulette playing cards in one drawer or other. Guests will go so far as to assume their presence and await a game of Old Maid after dinner, being entirely flabbergasted and possibly insulted if no such thing happens. The truth of it is that Gregory and every master Landaulette card maker before him has maintained an energetic secret charity whereby for every pack they sell another is sorcerously sent to a home in need. No one know the exact method by which this happens, except the master and their apprentices, all of whom are bound by deep magic to never share their proprietary magic on pain of abrupt miraculous abduction. Even the master maker doesn't know where they go.  
  
## Pitt Street: Pitt Rope (3)  
Dingad Drum    Lock    8/10/2/0    J4 (3)    rope-maker  
Dingad is a salty sailor turned rope maker after losing his good leg. However he has never actually been to the humpbacked sky, instead he secretly hoards penny adventure novels and delights in retelling them as original tales of his life before becoming a rope maker. He lost his leg in a rigging accident as an apprentice.  
  
## Blue Lane: Powerful Potent Strong (4)  
Reclusive Ceolfrid    Skeletal Counsel (3)     7/17/2/0    J4 (4)    flour merchant  
Ceolfrid used to be a competitive cobblestone tosser, champion in five counties, and assigns his success to his patented grain drink. It fires the belly, girds the loin, and engorges the lean meat of the limbs! He sells his dry mixture by the bag (just add milk) and remains in excellent shape and boisterous mood despite his age. A testament to the restorative power of grain.  
  
## Blue Lane: Meeting Hall of the Alliance for Lewd Liberties  (5)  
Punctilious Redgrave Presence 6/4/2/3 Atheist  
  
The clubhouse for the Alliance for Lewd Liberties is a single floor building with one large room and a staggeringly tall spire atop. It was once the tower of a larger building complex, but as time passed and the city grew all except the top floor and its spire were buried. Redgrave, the founder of the club, believes the gods' monopoly on ambrosia is tyrannical and must be broken. His members have infiltrated religious orders and intend to be excellent worshippers so as to get into the afterlife and bring it down from within.  
  
Initially the Alliance for Lewd Liberties acquired the building because it was affordable and no one else wanted it, but over time they have discovered ways down into the buried segments of the tower and, in turn, into the sewers and undercity. Not only does this provide more storage for placards and disguises, it also means their secret agents can meet with the club without risking discovery.
