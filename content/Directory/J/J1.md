## Ripple Road: The Bitter Ewery (1)  
Tanner  
Fabian Fruit 5/18/3/0      
Purple Lens (1)        J1 (1)  
Exotic leathers from beyond the sky, so the sign goes. There are skins of all shapes and textures unrecognisable to anyone other than the proprietor Fabian Fruit who spins tales of giants and sea creatures and demons being felled just for this sliver of prime skin. Fruit, dressed head to toe in leather versions of things that have no right to be leather, is a swindler and a salesman. His skins are fakes almost without exception, cunninly dyed and stamped to have otherworldy forms.   
  
## Ripple Road: Mother's Gyve (2)  
furrier  
Mother Aibhe    Disguise    7/11/2/0    J1 (2)      
  
In contrast to her neighbour, Mother Aibhe's furs are of a bewildering variety and all real. As soon as she catches your eye she will have picked up a prime unicorn pelt and be stroking it under your nose insisting you inspect the quality. The stock changes regularly butanything could turn up here, she has deep connections with the sphere hoping hunter fraternities and takes first call on everything they bring home.  
  
## Ripple Road: Shrine of St Ethelburga the Attentive (3)  
Augustus Melvin      
Strength    3/5/3/0    J1 (3)    priest  
Say St. Ethelburga's name three times and she'll come and take your children! So the children say, and as such they steer clear of her shrines. The largest complex to the saint in the city, a centre for her worship, and pilgrimmages by tired parents seeking a break (or more permenent relief). The outdoors statue of the Saint flaying children with a sapling keeps all but the boldest gangs at bay, and those get chased off by the bellowing Augustus and his leaded broom.
