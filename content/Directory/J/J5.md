## Pitt Street: Lust in Residence  
Habitual Lust    7/3/1/0    Cone of Air    J5 (1)        Air pilot  
This narrow townhouse built in the pre-war style of giant-blown glass on brass belongs to the flying ace Habitual Lust. He started his career as a kite scout at the age of 4 and from there has piloted everything from cruise aerostats to plasmic fighting torpedos. He is now elderly but has lost none of his verve and is locally known for throwing hot pennies from his balcony at the local gamins. Despite this he is idolised by them.  
  
## Pitt Street: Shotgun Spinneret   
Bertha Slug    7/13/2/0    Affix (3)     J5 (2)        stretcher of wool cloth  
This open fronted shop is manned by the smiling Bertha, the silk maker. The street carries the click click clicking of her spinning machines up and down, and she is always chatting to someone or other. Once inside the shop one can see the high roof, in whose rafters giant worms are suspended from many harpoons piercing their sides. The wounds are healed with bright orange scabs which occasionally drip into carefully located buckets below. From each of their spinnerets extends the grey silk that Bertha spins, pulled from them by the clicking machines.   
  
## Behind the Plaza of Time: Scavenger Pile OE23 (2)  
Toe Theodard    Exchange Shape (5)    6/3/2/0    J5 (3)    seller of scrap (old) iron  
A tall building crenellated with brightly painted freight cranes. The night scavengers bring their best finds here to be scrapped. Old bathtubs, pipes, railings, horse shoes, anything metal will be happily taken up by the operators working under Scrap Boss Theodard. Just pop what you have in the cradle and the crane will weigh and value it on the way up before tossing it into the hollow centre of the building and onto the growing pile. During the day they open the metal shutters and let the smelters and opportunists come to rummage through the rusty pile.  
  
## Behind Pitt Street: The Royal Over (3)  
Carthach Trumpetband    Thunder (2)    6/16/2/0    J5 (4)    baker  
Carthach used to be the imperial baker in the court of the Pillar King until he was exiled for dissident baking. Himself a pillar person, anyone ignorant of their ways might think no one works in the busy kitchen, and that this gold flecked concrete pillar by the oven is out of place. It is in fact Carthach, and like all pillar people is unable to move while observed. Have faith in their ways, buy the bread by the front, give the dough boy his penny, and enjoy your excellent, reasonably priced (though slightly gritty) bread.  
  
## Behind the Plaza of Time: The Hidden Friend  
Fred Author    5/17/2/0    Web (2)    J5 (5)        flask-maker  
The shop looks to the uninitiated like a junk shop, objects of all sorts clutter the place in no seeming order. There is, however, and overwhelming aroma of booze. Fred, the proprieter, certainly accounts for some of the smell. Round and redfaced, smiling absently all the time, festooned with hipflasks hiding in every pocket of his tightly fitted dinner suit. No, the smell comes from everywhere, for the shop is in fact selling hidden flasks, everything is full of booze from the books to the statues to the wax candles. Fill them up, hide them in plain sight  
  
## Behind the Plaza of Time: Gladness & Co.  
Everhard Gladness    3/5/1/0    Poison    J5 (6)        Architect  
Everhard, heir to the Gladness shellac fortune, runs a small architectural firm out of the living room of his period townhouse. Using his bottomless funds he has hired the finest, and most tolerant, drafstmen and planners the town has to offer. Every few months Everhard gets a new idea for an improbable project involving impossible materials or demolishing swathes of the city and his staff dutifully oblige him in drawing up plans and arranging meetings with suppliers and bureaucrats. He stands no hope of ever getting a project commissioned, but his reckless spending of the family fortune on materials and location scouting is good for the local economy and so is generally encouraged.
