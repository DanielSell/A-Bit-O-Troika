# H3  
## Ripple Road: Hollowhead Victuals (1)  
Ictus Hysterisus 5/12/1/1  
Skill: Tunnel Fighting  
  
Bald, golden toothed Ictus runs the grocers all hours. No one knows his secret. Under his smock he hides more gold additions, received during the Oblation Wars, which he never talks about except on the feast of St. Michael when he and the shop boys drink out on the street until they can't move.  
  
## Tallow Street: Caped Up To Fattings' (2)  
Friend Thorfinn    Quench (1)    3/13/1/1    H3 (2)    cloak maker  
Cormac Alfwold    Affix    6/9/3/2    H3 (2)    mercer  
  
Home of the pieboy cape, so called because of its pockets being big enough and numerous enough to carry a family's worth of eel pies. Usage for pie carrying is not recommended, Cormac the elderly shopboy will say, but the repairs on grease stained pocket linings does help keep coal in the fire so they won't fight you on it. Friend Thorfinn makes his capes in the window of the shop where crowds gather to watch on sunny days.   
  
## Ripple Road: Homodomo (3)  
Corebus Riskyfang    Ride    6/17/1/0    H3 (3)    Candle maker  
  
The shop is full. No more candles can fit, but Corebus doesn't stop making them. Open all hours, they spill out onto the street, lamplit drifts of candles by night. A huge man, knotted and scarred, even the scented wax doesn't quite cover the distant smell of blood about him. He serves customers with a solomn kindness, never hiding his desire to get back to work. Gossip goes that he's cursed for a lide of misdeeds. No one steals the candles, even when they roll down the road. Even the gamins call him 'boss' and keep it polite.  
  
## Ripple Road: Arms Forth (4)  
Austremonius Dress    Fire Bolt (1)    5/15/1/0    H3 (4)    Skinners[a] (fur traders)  
  
This pub is always almost empty and unusually loud. Austremonius, a sinuous man, hunched and eager like a coati, owns the establishment and holds no holds barred bull fights in the basement, from which the raucous noise issues in waves. The upstairs is manned by a rotating cast of tourists looking for casual work and serves an excellent stake.   
  
## Ripple Road: The Buccal Bell (5)   
Eveness Untoward    Befuddle    3/18/4/0    H3 (5)    Bell-maker  
The foundry has been given over to the hammering out of a single magnificent bell for the last decade. Eveness is the second generation to work on it, after an unfortunate accident while attaching the clapper; their mother hasn't been able to lift a tuning hammer since, and so lives in the apartments above, haunted by the ringing. The shrine of St. Hethor sends a regular stipend to fund the project, and are happy to do so so long as their exacting standards are met and the deadline for the resurrection of St. Hethor is not missed..
