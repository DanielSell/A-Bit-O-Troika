# H8  
## Graisle Street: Shrine of St. Hathor (1)  
Caterina Glum    Helping Hands (1)    4/3/3/1    H8 (1)    weaver  
  
The rooftop of this busy shrine is full of spires, each with its own bell dangling in full view. Between them fly the disciples of the saint, each equipped with plasmic powered feathered wing apparatuses and a hammer, with which they ring the bells at the alloted times. One large tower stand conspicuously empty, and the cherubic first diciple Caterina will be happy to explain that this is where the final bell will go, the bell which will wake the sleeping body of Saint Hathor who slumbers beneath this very ground! You will be encouraged to toss a penny into a yawning pit which ultimately leads to the saints resting place. This will help pay for the bell and ensure mercy when Hathor awakens.  
  
## Graisle Street: Lollop House (2)  
Eugene Liberato    Swim    5/12/4/2    H8 (2)    Frutier  
  
Lollop House used to be a bottling workshop run by gin smugglers. Though the scourge of gin has long been wiped out, the smuggler tunnels remain. Eugene, a ruddy old fellow with a penchant for designer wigs, uses them nightly to skulk beneath his neighbours houses and listen to conversations. He doesn't know yet but the tunnels lead all the way into the Anti-City beneath Troika.   
  
## Graisle Street: Brool Towers (3)  
Indigo Gohardus    Posthumous Vitality (5)    3/17/3/0    H8 (3)    maker of armour plating  
  
Actually the ground floor of a dismantled city wall tower, the iron stairs still go up some twenty feet above the roof, allowing for excellent views. Indigo is a necromancer of small fame, best known for his short lived zombie theatre some years ago.
