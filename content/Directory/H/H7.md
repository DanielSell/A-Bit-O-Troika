## Tun Road: Cranrat's Requisites for the Table (2)  
First Prelate Hebephren 5/5/2/0  
Spell: Presence  
  
Paper dishes, plates, pie collars, doyleys and cups hand cut and decorated for all occasions. A cramped reception has examples of the fine craftsmanship of their cutters, displayed on cluttered shelves and in ranks of bins. The staff are proud of their ancient craft, and are the 5th generation exiled theocratic government of a distant world set on spreading their credo and building their power with indoctrinating patterns.
