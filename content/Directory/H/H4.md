# H4  
## **Ugly Granny Medicine**  
_Ripple Road #1  
_Residents:_ **_Gertrude Tenenan, early morning drysalter_** _(Spell: Exchange Shape) 5/17/2/1(clothes stuffed with newspapers)_ - **_Weird Ina, late night herbalist_** _(Spell: Tongue Twister) 3/15/2/1(clothes stuffed with newspapers) -_ **_Adam Chapel, nervous chemist_** _(Spell: Presence) 8/9/1/0_   
    
Jars of powders, unguents, pickles and balms fill high, tight corridors of shelves that never lead to a counter at which to buy them. Adam Chapel, the chemist, though clearly heard rattling glass and shuffling papers one aisle over, remains unseen and always one step ahead of you as though afraid of being caught. Consulting him about an ailment can be done by talking as though to oneself, wondering aloud at the unfairness of your condition and how you would be able to go home and rest if only someone could ease your immediate and clearly listed symptoms. Shortly the correct medicine along with a receipt will appear in a paper bag poking out of a shelf around the next corner. Anyone leaving without paying will receive a visit from a couple of unusually large alcades soon after.
  
## Ripple Road: Jasper's Fine Mare (2)  
Jasper Moredew 5/14/2/0  
Spell: Peace  
  
Stable and freehouse, room for several animals and their riders. The bar is a converted box stall and is quite snug. No staff, only Jasper, 12p/day stabling, 22p/day for an all-you-can-drink mug. Self-service to the home-brewed alcohol. It is think, white & sour, of mild potency. Jasper is an exotic middle aged man, unplacable accent, draws from a seemingly endless list of stories and interesting or suspiciously convenient customs from the old world. Many conflicting. Will never say how or where he makes the booze. Pouch of 12fl 32p hidden in the hay loft. Daily changing arcane chalk marks on the walls, assured to be an old tradition from the old world.  
  
  
  
## Ripple Road: Hart's Horn (3)  
Aureata Moistblanket 4/6/1/0  
Skill: Poison  
  
Seller of fine soft foods for the sick, young, elderly and lazy. Huge glass tubes filled with mashed meals dissapear into the upper floor where they are refilled. The contents are homogenous and colourful. Aureata fills bottles for customers from requested tubes but will refuse demands to mix them together. Each meal is 6p, or 5p if you bring your own bottle.

## Red Lane: Empasm House (5)  
George the Piper. 6/17/1/0  
Skill: Mathmology  
  
A master multi-instrumentalist who has made it his life's mission to record and perform the music transmitted between the stars across the dark waves of the humpbacked sky. His music is highly technical but mostly unlistenable to an uneducated ear. Its performance and practise also undermines the hypostasis and can initiate spontaneous psychic osmosis in the listener. Most of George's previous band mates are now raving loonies, amorphous beasts, or psychopomps.
