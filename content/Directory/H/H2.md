## Ormon's Corner: Royal Copyists Charter (1)  
Associate Officer for the Supervision of the Collation and Amendment of Texts, Jackanori Spendfellow 6/13/2/0  
Spell: Poison  
  
Spendfellow has the look of being made from old newspapers. Bereft of any discernible personality. Dangerously ambitious. An oak office with many copyists at many desks. Piles of paper, puddles of ink, quiet but frantic activity. From 8p per page, from 20p if editing or legal consultation required. Customers are impatient public servants, university students and owners of lost animals.  
  
  
  
## Tallow Street: Maols Occupancy (2)  
Overlord Juliana Maol (3/6/3/0)  
Spell: Purple Lens  
  
The palatial ancestral home of the Maol Overlords has been split in to multiple small flats occupied by labourers, apprentices and shop assistants. The current Overlord resides in what used to be the lumber room, still packed solid with old furniture and dust sheets. She is dressed in golden furs with the sleeves most often rolled up and wears the opal crown of her line held in place with copious hat pins. She is old, severe, and vital.

## Ripple Road: Deadman's Knell (3)  
Will Silver    Brittle Twigs    8/4/1/1    H2 (3)    Doctor  
  
The traditional residence of the senior consultant of St. Orman's Asylum is a white washed cottage which bulges like an overripe tomato. Doctor Silver is a celebrated physician and wizard, quickly approaching his 80th year in the position. Unlike his patients, he does not shy away from the miasma of Troikan air, often leaving his windows and doors wide open when he is home. Visitors are received generously, and those seeking personal treatment from the good doctor might be lucky enough to be invited to his personal clinic in the cottage sub-basement.   
  
## Ormon's Corner: St. Ormon's Asylum (4)  
Eva Admiration    6/15/3/1    Cockroach (5)    H2 (4)        administrator of hospital  
  
St Ormon's approach to health is one of strict control and the belief that healthy and happy is the default state of the universe. If a patient is removed from toxic environments then health will be all that is left, invariably, or so their philosophy goes. In practice this takes the form of varying degrees (and corresponding costs) of isolation ranging from a few days in air tight apartments pumped with imported breathable gasses, all the way to long term subterranean silver-lined, lead panelled, hermetically sealed, and fully life supported encystments. The higher level confinements are tightly tuned magical constructions, so there are as many magical as medical doctors on staff. Doctor Admiration, chief of staff at St. Ormon's, for instance pioneered the use of cockroaching the patients in to tortoises to enable more compact and cost efficient seasonal encystments.
