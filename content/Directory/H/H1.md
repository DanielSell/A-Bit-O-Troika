# H1  
## Clatoyant Road: Edoxia House (1)  
Residence  
Derfel-Gadarn Edoxia    9/14/2/1      
Skill: Astrology, Golden Barge Pilot  
  
The home of Derfel-Fadarn is currently empty, and remains so for years at a time while he sails the humpbacked sky. The halls of his manse are haunted by dust-cloth covered furniture all of intensly Troikan design with not much more sign of his proffession and travels besides several paintings of his ship, *The Lacuna* and a safe of exotic design. A private security firm patrol the manse every few hours, armed with clubs and hunting owls. The safe contains 100,000p in spider bank bonds and three astronomical maps leading to a specific place on distant spheres. It would require an expert navigator to decipher it, and even they wouldn't be able to tell you that two of the maps are decoys leading to extreme danger.     
  
## Behind Ormon's Corner: Dandiprat House (2)  
Holiday home  
Otto Wishproot    8/15/3/3 (2)      
Spell: Thought Vapour (1)  
  
Otto is travelling the city on an extended tour. He comes from the vast estates of Gascoigne where he oversees a criminal enterprise whereby he strips out Council utilities and robs those sent to repair them, like his father before him and so on. He is large, bald, bright red, and accutely aware of the social position his accent marks him with. To compensate he maintains a confrontational attitude and wears comically fancy clothes into which he has stitched the copper plates of his proffesion.  
  
  
## Ormon's Corner: The Disomus (3)  
Stonecarver  
Sweltering Tiburtius    5/9/4/0      
Spell: See Through (1)    H1 (3)          
The door to this shop is carved in the shape of a two bodied, single headed, naked man. The two bodies throw punches across the door gap as the face howls in silent rage and confusion. Tiburtius' masterpiece attracts a lot of customers and ensures he is always busy. His work is organic, repulisive, and obscurely political, making them perfect conversaion pieces for lounges and dining halls. Tiburtius himself a shy and innapropriately loud, followed by wild rumours of leisure time proclivities but with no proof. True or not, they are great for business.  
  
  
## Ormon's Corner: The Georgician (4)  
Fleece seller  
Soter Cross 6/13/1/2      
Spell: Ember (2)    H1 (4)          
  
Smothered by piles of fleece, the muted sound of a lyre meets customers to Soter's shop. His voice and playing are earthy, and put you in mind of open skies and long nights on a hillside. He is far too old to fend off thylocenes so he now only sells the fruit of the flock and offers no opinion.  
  
## Ormon's Corner: The Pit of Ormon (5)  
Cheese maker  
Slayer Decline    7/10/2/1      
Farseeing (2)     H1 (5)      
  
Sitting over the street's namesake, ex-maurauder Slayer Decline has conquered the foul pit of Ormon. He's rid it of evil, routed the Commonwealth holdouts, sealed the entrances to the underworld (he swears), and discovered it's excellent humidity and bacterial biome primed for making uniquely excellent cheese. The proprietry cheese, Ormon's Frab, is dark red, slightly translucent, and marbled with crumbly black mould. It is traditionally eaten cold with boiled eel, the taste is nutty, metalic, and lingering.

## Behind Ripple Road: The Sign of the Musnud (2)  
Tinsmith  
Publicanus Face      
Skill: Healing    4/5/2/0    I1 (2)      
The roof of this tinsmithery holds an oversized throne made entirely of tin. The workmanship is average but the thing is huge and very old. It is covered in owl droppings and pollution carried down with the rain. A local fascination, people often try to sneak up there and sit on it, but are thwarted by barbed wire along the entire building and Publicanus' accurately thrown gobs of scrap tin. Gamins say it's the seat of the king of Troika and whoever sits in it takes the crown. Monarchists have stayed quiet on the issue, unwilling to look foolish by following childish games, but they're secretly intrigued. Some mornings Publicanus has so send a coal boy up on a ladder with a broom to push off another wild eyed corpse of someone fool enough to ignore the signs and sit in the chair.   
  
## Behind Ripple Road: The Ignavia (3)  
Upholsterers  
Shriveled Alfrick  
5/14/2/2    Astral Reach (1)    I1 (3)  
During the day Alfrick is a smooth, well dressed, effortlessly charismatic, geuinely kind, community minded salesman and master upholsterer. By night he and select friends roughly sew one amongst them into soft furniture and dump them in random spots in the city. This behavior is an open secret and tolerated by the locals, though they are careful not to pick up or sit in any chairs they find on the side of the road, much to Alfrick's dismay.
