## Blanket Road: The Copper Sygnet   
Clock Controller    5/2/2/2    Illusion (2 per viewer)    D6 (2)        coppersmith/kettle maker  
  
Once famous for its automatons, the Copper Sygnet now makes perfunctory kitchenware. The old Master Yardward died over a year ago of a sudden fit of agedness yet his finest creation, Clock Controller, continues to make basic items day and night. Clock Controllr's key whirs with no sign of stopping, and Master Yardward lays there still on the floor, worse for ware. Neighbours and alcalde have tried to enter and remove the body but the automaton has reacted violently to their efforts. People reason that the machine can't go on forever, and the master can't die any further, so people are largely happy to let the spectacly continue, much to the enjoyment of visitors.  
  
## **Onwards! Die! Die!**    
_Behind Blanket Road_  
_Residents_: **_Yohan Manor_** _(Spell: Ember, Lancemaking) 6/12/1/4_   
    
Named after a quote from the famous war hero and martyr St Sir Ormon, this lance makers yard serves as a gathering space for rowdy jousters and monarchists. Yohan, master lance maker and aspiring revolutionary, lords over the place like a Duke (he would never presume kingship), handing down proclamations on jousting fashion and commentary on the results of the latest tournaments. He wears full tournament armour at all times, both to intimidate customers and to hide his cacogenic form.
