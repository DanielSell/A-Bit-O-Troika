## Familiarity Way: Beauch House (1)  
Mortified Beauch 5/5/4/0      
Locks  
  
Beauch is a mule dealer of small success and stature. His distinctive froglike features and many jointed fingers cause rumours of undine blood. He lives in the remains of his family fortune and fears his dwindling resources will cause him to lose his manse and reveal the submerged tunnels he has kept safe and hidden for so long.

Jacobus, with deep scars on his forehead and a wandering eyepatch, runs the cutlers out front and keeps riffraff and alcalde from finding their way into the sealed, refirdgerated thinking engine workshop in the cellar. Tress, who runs the workshop, is a hierodule from the higher spheres and possesses the knowledge not just of engine repair but of the creation of entirely new thinking engines. If it weren't for Jacobus' protection she would have been captured long ago.  
  
## Familiarity Way: The Measure (3)  
Vitalis Shovel    6/3/2/0

Wooden cabinets line the walls and create passageways through the store. All are labelled and each contain varieties of weights and measures, in all kinds and methods one could possibly hope to find. Stadardised yardsticks, palms, firkins, chains, poles, grains, feet, inches, pints, furlongs, shekels, metres, transits, carobs, tons, stones, cubits, all can be found here with garuntees of accuracy (if stored in appropriate conditions) and at reasonable prices.   
  
  
## Tallow Street: The Side Saddle (4)  
Uriel Scrape    6/3/1/0      
Jolt (1)    D2 (4)        Saddlers  
The corner of Tallow Street and Familiarity Way is dominated by the smell of leather polish coming from the Side Saddle, a building whose ground floor has been almost entirely removed in order to make space for riding animals to enter the store directly. Uriel, the proprietor, grabs saddles and harnesses off hooks with a stick in rapid succession, bombarding the customer with choices until they relent or flee. Locals know her ways and try to distract her patter by making small talk about her sister, giving them enough time to examine the stock in peace.
