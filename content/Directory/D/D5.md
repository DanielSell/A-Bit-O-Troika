# D5  
## Hypotherm Circus: The Ephemerid Tower NOT FINISHED (1)  
Uncumber the Apocalypse Saint (6/8/4/0)  
Spell: Zed  
Catchy-Carry (3/18/1/0)  
Strength  
  
The tower is a shell around a multi story aquarium containing a cetacean apocalypse saint. Gantries curl around it giving access to supplicants consulting the saint and excellent views of the walls and ceiling, which are regularly re-painted with educational frescoes regarding the hollowing out of infinity and the impending end of the city. Uncumber is prone to hours long lectures with little provocation and will often leave his tank and take them to the streets carried on the bent back of Catchy-Carry.
