# D1  
## Sagun Row: Dipsum's Colluvia (1)  
Once a huge inn, wood panneled and stone floored, but now abandoned. By night shadowy people meet in the old rooms, talking is often heard within, but no one is seen to come or go. Even by day the local gamins fear venturing inside.  
  
## Comfit Street #2
### Most Beautiful Age 
Phocas Cloud 4/12/4/1(many jumpers)  
Spell: Darksee  
Old clothes are neatly stacked from floor to ceiling in this tiny shop. The whole of it can be seen from the windows, up to the smiling figure of Phocas deep at the back steaming and folding clothes. Inside the smell of countless people, well washed, permeates.  
  
## Familiarity Way #3
### Fencing annex of St Burkard's Horticultural School for People (3)  
Danger Domnina    8/16/1/0  
Web, Sword Fighting   
  
As the students of St Burkard's grew in prestige the donations piled up on the small school. In a consultation where no one quite remembers the proceedings, the logic went that were they to gather new plants from further afield it would become essential that all students could defend themselves against any and all autochthonous flora and fauna. The savings in armed guides alone would justify the expense! The school is overseen by Danger Domnina, retired lawyer of Yongardy and honorary botanist, who has a youthful anger and thick scars which hide her advanced age.  
  
  
  
## Familiarity Way: St Burkard's Horticultural School for People (4)  
Phocas Beoc    7/2/4/0  
Sleight of hand          
  
A small but highly regarded college for the training of botanists, gardeners, tree surgeons and florists. The ancient Phocas Beoc has been headmaster of the school for a score of generations and shows no inclination to retire. The students are devoted to their old headmaster and have become an accidental society of mutual cooperation and institutional intimidation surounding him, making the graduates of St Burkard's a disproportionate fixture of the most esteemed horticultural positions across the city.
