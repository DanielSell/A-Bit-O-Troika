# D3  
## Rodolio Square: Dever House  (1)  
Panemotis Jacopino    7/3/2/0    (1)    
brittle twigs        Duelist    
  
"Hup! Ho!" *splat* echoes through Rodolio Square every morning from the open window (no matter the weather) of Dever House. Master Jacopino is there with his backsword, on the wide boards of his personal gymnasium, attacking bulging sacks of offal tied to posts. The bare chested old man is sun beaten and heavily scarred so that he resembles the trussed and undulating meats on his floor. His huge grey beard is tied up upon his head while he trains or fights, which is most of the time. When not training he is relaxing on his roof reading and drinking coffee. Anyone trying to contact the eminent duelist without going through his fixer is likely to fall foul of his sword, since he will believe you to be an agent of one of his many enemies. Almachius Twinkle, owner of the The Rhubarb and Custard, is his handler though he makes no advert of it. For a price he will arrange for Panemotis to challenge and kill whoever you like.  
  
## Rodolio Square: Grith House (2)  
Nathanael Dingad    Run    5/7/2/0    D3 (2)    sewer of cloth  
  
The Dingad linen fortune has been invested in what many suppose to be the largest collection of hand sculptures in the city. The house is an unofficial museum, viewable by appointment only, and can be easily spotted by the weathered limestone hands shielding every window and door like a golfer searching after their ball. The last living heir, young master Nathanael, lives in the hollow of a plaster fist and always has a few hundred thousand pence on hand in case a potential purchase appears unexpectedly.  
  
  
## Behind Rodolio Square: The House of Silence (3)  
Livinus Silence    5/2/2/1    Gills (3)    D3 (3)        doctor [of law]  
  
Livinus a barrister, theurgist, and the great grandson of the famous devil-cleper Ulricke Silence, who made his families fortune invoking demon spies for a short lived Troikan tyrant. After the fall of the tyrant the family bought forgiveness by transferring their secret police to the Universal Congress and promising to not leave town unto the 6th generation. They have followed the exact letter of the pardon, never once leaving the city or brooking with demons, however they have since set up a lucrative network of gelatinous eye-beasts who slither through the pipes and sewers of the city gathering intelligence on behalf of their masters. The House of Silence's services are exclusive and expensive, requiring an introduction and careful vetting for Congress spies, along with tens of thousands of pence in compensation.  
  
## Rodolio Square: Sand Crutch Villa (4)  
Fortunatus Gall    4/6/3/3    Disguise    D3 (4)        Potter  
  
On this plot of land once stood the winter palace of St. Osgyth, a two hundred foot tower of seemless schist from atop which he would hurl apostates into the square below. Aquired by the Galls not long ago, they quickly cut it down to a nub and built the current open plan villa on top of it for one of their prodigal heirs, Fortunatus. He spends his days spinning pottery on his balcony in the nude, regularly tossing failed pieces over the edge while laughing at the objections from below with "I think you'll find it's an ecumenical matter!" Speculation around the motivation for demolishing the tower is rife, public opinion is universally negative, and Fortunatus is loving it.
