## Hypotherm Circus: Vigor's Fancy Dress (1)  
Midnight Bite    6/16/2/0    Fear (1)      
  
Dusty and packed solid with boxes of clothes, wigs, make-up and props. Midnight, the proprietor, sits at the back of the shop and is more often experienced as a jolly and disembodied voice than seen in person. There are no labels, and he is too large to fit between the stacks, so Midnight shouts directions to what you are looking for. After hours the shop is open, by appointment only, to backstreet duellists looking to be fitted for the animalistic duelling masks so popular amongst their sort.  
  
  
## Ironspike Lane: Hamid House (2)  
Hubert Acrates    5/16/2/2(knows the future)    Mirror Selves (3)    Astrologer  
  
The astrologist, Hubert Acrates, gained fame and a modest fortune by discovering a mirror-city in the stars, a perfect reverse copy in every way of Troika. This discovery, and the ensuing discussion of which city is the real city, is now widely used by local councils as a scapegoat for shody infrastructure and chaotic town planning decisions. The mirror city did it.  
  
  
## Hypotherm Circus: On The Three Natures (3)  
Viator Deusdedit    Ironhand (3)    5/4/2/0    D4 (3)    bowl-maker  
  
A notorious turtling parlour, where the roughest sorts come to get body modifications on the cutting edge of fashion. If you see someone hanging out on a bridge with flesh spikes and a hair-trigger snapping jaw, playing loud shell-music, chances are they come here, the centre of their barely tolerated sub-culture. Viator, the pioneering owner, turtler and shell drummer, is a tiny old lady with no visible modifications. Her customers joke that she started off as a turtle and worked backwards. She never corrects them.  
  
## Hypotherm Circus: The Advent Anex  (4)
Miriam Felcite    Teleport    9/17/1/0    D4 (4)    Dyer  
  
A minor tourist attraction, the last nub of an ancient city wall. The brickwork is so worn that it is held up by rusty iron struts with old signs suggesting the city is aware and on their way to fix it. The top of the wall is a small apartment, once sone sort of defensive nest, occupied by the explorer Miriam Felcite. The only way to enter the flat is to climb the wall and enter through a window. There has been a lot of coming and going recently, since Ms. Felcite is interviewing for a crew to help her find the mythical island of Mount Analog.
