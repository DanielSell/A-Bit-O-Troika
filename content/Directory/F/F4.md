## Ormon's Square: Production of Intellect (1)  
Abraham Senorina    Cone of Air    9/14/3/0    F4 (1)    Tailor  
Bulging glass display windows of this shop are filled with ragged, hugely expensive, outfits. Abraham is a short, heavily powdered, man dressed in examples of his work. The visionary tailor has employed the services of a goat with no name who, under his guidance, customises pieces of clothing by nibbling on them. Once they are finished they are stabilised by hand and put up for sale. He has an extensive list of fashionable clients so nothing stays in the shop for very long.  
  
## Ironspike Lane: Seven Heavenly Rulers Tumbling Club (2)  
Jeanne Sancta    6/17/3/1 (tough as old boots)    Sleight of Hand        Tumbling instructor  
  
Ms. Sancta was a famous acrobat in her prime, but now she is half blind, half deaf, and usually confined to a low stool upon which she perches like a spider, folded below a forest of legs and knees and walking sticks. The local gamin gangs thing she's oblivious to the fact that they use her tumbling classes as a training ground for promising burglars and roof-runners but while she may look knackered, she is as sharp as ever. Ms. Sancta sees her class as a chance to reform delinquents, and accepts that most will slip through her net. Her crew of coarse assistant instructors stand as a testament to the success of her campaign.  
  
  
## Ample Street: The Charitable Company of Key-Keepers (3)  
Jacobo Glum    Climb    9/7/2/0    F4 (3)      
  
The doors and shutters of this building have large, exotic locks, many with no clear way of inserting a key or removing them. This and a small plaque next to the door mark it as the workshop of a group of clavigers of renown. Each are famous and independently wealthy from years of treasure hunting and lucrative consultation jobs. Anyone with a locksmithing job of suitable interest and novelty can engage their services for free, though as the years go on they are needing to become more and more outlandish to provoke their attention. Once engaged they will bloodymindedly attack the problem until it is solved and will be stopped by no one, not even the one who engaged their services.  
  
  
## 4 Behind Ample Street: Florus' Metals  
Florus Dusk    5/8/4/0    Swim    SPARE        Scrap merchant  
  
Mr. Dusk is a a wiry stoat of a man, ambiguously old, smelling of brass polish and always looking over his shoulder. And quite rightly, since he is a notorious fence and gossip dealer. His scrap metal yard is a hangout for elder gamins hot on the heels of adulthood, who bring him the best gear and news from their younger peers. He keeps his business safe by having no great ambition and by engaging in low level blackmail. In all things he wishes for a quiet life. As such he keeps on polite terms with Ms. Sancta from across the way, and makes sure not to deal with members of her club lest her assistant coaches pay him a visit.
