## Comfit Street: Hall of the The Worshipful Company of Lightmongers (1)  
Bridle Zeal    8/5/2/0  
blood shroud        F1 (1)    Lightmonger  
  
A well and tastefully lit door in an otherwise blank and windowless townhouse leads to the dazzling halls of the lightmongers guild. Bridle Zeal is a full time lamplighter, responsible for keeping the thousands of lights in the windowless halls alight and ready at all times.   
  
## Behind Comfit Street: Lumber & So On (2)  
Helpful Tilli 3/15/3/1  
Healing        F1 (2)    Builders' Merchants  
A glorified shed, high and wide, with floor to ceiling logs and boards and other construction materials. Tilli is the yard master and eldest daughter of a huge family who who make up the staff of the building merchants yard. Each is large, snow white, effortlessly strong, and well mannered.  
  
## Comfit Street: The Oak Orb (3)  
Neot 7/3/2/0  
Thought Vapour        F1 (3)    Carpenter  
  
An army of apprentices help the gleeful Neot carve and paint his puzzles. He wanders from table to table pointing and adjusting, pulling brushes from behind his ear to touch up a jigsaw piece here and there. It's become quite popular to purchase his puzzle sculptures to hide wills and small gifts for loved ones in and watch them struggle to open it. A gift for everyone really.   
  
## Comfit Street: The Studio Of Paulus Prudentius Keep Out  
Paulus Prudentius 9/6/2/2  
Diminish (2)         F1 (4)    sculptor  
  
Paulus rarely goes out, but when he runs out of clay he scurries through the streets weighed down in armour pointing a huge knife in front of him to part the crowds. His sculptures are all ephemeral, whereby models and well-to-dos visit to be covered in wet clay. They emerge, slowly sloughing off clay mixed with feathers, sand, sticks and so on, and head to their funtions and galleries to demonstrate the latest work of Paulus Prudentius.
