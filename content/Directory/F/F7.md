## Behind Blanket Road: Hellkite Manor (1)  
Iron Shenute    Tracking    5/11/2/2    F7 (1)    casket maker  
Tomb robbery isn't as fashionable as it once was, but it is still ery much in mode to be buried as though you have a lot worth stealing. Iron Shenute produces cast iron caskets of a standard design, one size fits most, with unparralelled robber confounding features. Beyond their solid construction the have caustic vapour pockets throughout the frame, ensuring drillers meet a melty end, vacuum sealed solar salamanders which rehydrate on contact with air, and to top it all off the air inside is replaced with an explosive gas which turns any holes into searing gouts of fire. Industry leading security for the dead who can afford it.  
  
## Behind Blanket Road: The Cat's Chechia (2)  
Mike Brunch    Blood Shroud (4)    6/10/3/2    F7 (2)    linen seller  
  
The plaza betwen The Cat's Checia and the Temple of All Gods is decorated with the goods of Checia, strung like heavy bunting from wall to wall in folly zigzags. The shop itself is floor to ceiling rolls of cloth, so Mike, a bald amphora shaped man, sits outside on a stool and enjoys the weather no matter its variety.    
  
## Behind Blanket Road: The House of Urane (3)  
Magnobodus Urane    Crafting skills    6/15/4/0    F7 (3)    Oven worker  
  
Magnobodus, a freelance oven stoker of means, spends most of his days hiding in his sitting room, the rest of his house being taken over by seperatist goblins from the Brown Labyrinth. They turned up one day and started installing twisting false walls throughout the house, leaving him with only one room for himself. They leave him be, and pay rent by stuffing crude golden coins under the door every Bathday.  
  
## Behind Tun Road: Ondatra! Ondatra!  
Anvil Narcissus    Trapping    7/12/2/2    F7 (4)    Grocers (spice merchants)  
  
Narcissus wears the defunct uniform of the never built tram service that was meant to pass throguh the plaza of time, wether through sentimental city pride or a desire to be unusually unusual he won't say. Regardless it makes him look like a resting moth, or a face seen in the curves of tree bark. Ondatra! Ondatra! specialises in Troikan spice from distant boroughs, barrels of them spill out onto the street advertising their quality. Powdered cones, dry silphium, pulverised black-brick, chalk, juniper berries, alley slime, owl down, wine musk, and so on.
