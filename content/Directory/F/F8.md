## Tun Road: Cannons of Repute As Used By The State  
Calamity James    Ironhand (3)    8/9/1/0    F8 (1)    Gunmakers  
James, a gnarled pyrimidical man, is a maker of cannons. With the abolition of the military he had to improvise, and thus the hobby of cannoneering was born. Through a campaign of sponsored executions, his personal cannons became a desirable item to be taken with the family on short inter borough holidays, where they would be fired into lakes to skip canonballs across the water, or animal carcasses would be stuffed in the barrels and fired up into the air to attract swooping owls. The craze has died down but the habbit has formed and his shop does well.   
  
## Tun Road: Contus Crater (2)  
Sargeant Damhnade Fragrance    explode    8/14/3/0    F8 (2)      
  
The crater is a steep pit left over from the war. The bottom is filled with a black sludge, anyone can slide down it safely but getting back out requires a spiraling run up the trecherous and ever crumbling sides. The sludge is deep and winds off into channels and vaults of pitch black slime. A career can be made of diving these depths and retrieving the concretions that litter them. Many, but not all contain treasure. Cut gems, goblets, gold coins, crowns, fine furs, wines of obscure vintage. Some are just a couple of spoons or a brass hatpin. In times past the divers would compete viciously for space in the pit, diving time and time again on their secret routes, returning to take bids from wealthy citizens standing around the lip above the bottomless filth. The heated activity ensured the crater grew year on year, crumbling under foot. Fears of it eating up the neighbourhood grew and the Alcalde stepped in and laid claim to the pit in the name of the Universal Congress. Now all divers pay to receive a diving licence and in turn are paid a flat fee of 100p per concretion wherupon their goods are taken to the public auction block. Damhnade is the gelatenous Alcalde sergant overseeing the issuing of licences and the compensation of the divers. His corruption is obvious but he keeps the gamin gangs out and enough money flowing to keep the commander uninterested in changing the situation.  
  
## The Contus Crater Brown Labyrinth Entrance (3)   
Aching Dominican-Slaughter    Evaluate    8/7/4/0    F8 (3)    cheese-maker  
  
The crater entrance to the brown labyrinth is busy with the cheese-seller Dominican-Slaughter exchanging goods from cart to cart in rapid shouted exchanges with some goblins. The process looks to an outsider like a game of hungry hippos but there is an ages old system to it. Anyone getting close to them (or entering the labyrinth) will be included in the goblins bartering and have d3 items exchanged for d3 goblin goods.   
  
1. Bottle of pressed owl wine (one ration)  
2. A large gold coin with the face of the goblin king on  
3. Elbow root (damage as mace, or one ration)  
4. Live goblin organ, dirty and pulsating  
5. Goblin helmet menaced with spikes (armour +1, max 3. Damage as knife)  
6. Hard Troikan blue cheese  
  
## The Brown Labyrinth Entrance off Tun Road  
Coincidental Fissure Entanglement    Slide Skywards (6)    4/10/2/0    F8 (4)    master [builder]  
  
A thinking engine, Coincidental Fissure Entanglement, is being searched by four goblin guards on suspicion of spying. They are looking for construction plans and material recipes on the engine's person. Entanglement is indeed a spy stealing secrets and will stealthily stuff the rolled up microfilm plans in the pocket of any character who approaches within arms reach of him. Any guard finding this will arrest the party and take them deep within the labyrinth to be imprisoned in the goblin hoard. The microfilm reveals the secret of the hoard to those with the tools to view them, and those people (the Illustrious Guild of Meat Packers) will want them back.
