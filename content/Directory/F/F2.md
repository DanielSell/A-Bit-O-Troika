## The Serpentine Reserve: Poplation Adjustment Bureau (1)  
Reticent Alodia    6/9/1/2(unobstrusive stab-proof plates)  
Banish Spirit (6)  
  
This unpopular limb of the Universal Congress is housed in an old wartime fortress kept in good repair. The fortress is famous for its labyrinthine corridors and hidden rooms, which make it an imposing defensive structure and an exhausting place to work. The average population adjuster might expect to walk half a mile of corridors full of murder holes and false walls from the front gate to her desk. Alodia, always padded like a child in a blizzard, is the public relations officer of the bureau and can be occasionally coaxed out into the street to address journalists and angry citizens suspecting her people of sponsoring spates of murders or unexplained fires. To this her answer is always the same: "We are acutely aware of the pain that is to come. We will continue to do whatever is necessary".  
  
## Comfit Street: Vaults of the Guddled Pig (2)  
Paul Speechless    5/10/2/0    
Illusion (2 per viewer)    F2 (2)    iron worker  
  
From the street the Vaults of the Guddled Pig is a bundle of belching chimneys sticking out of the ground like a bunch of dead flower. Inside, around the foul vapours, the place descends to a sweltering basement full of vats of molten metal into which the scrap of the street is hurled. Paul Speechless is an old and suspicious man who rememebers bad times and expects them to come again and consequentially runs his crew of scavengers like they are always planning to do him ill. The rumours of his buried wealth do him no good, and ensure his paranoia is not totally unfounded.   
  
  
  
  
## Clatoyant Road: Flying Razors (3)  
Ethelgiva Persuader    9/9/3/0  
Exchange Shape  
  
Welded to the floor, her parts disseminated throughout the shop, Ethelgiva is an integral part of the building and has been for decades. Customers have come, man and boy, for the efficient administrations of the famous thinking engine barber. There is also fund to be had watching tourists duck and run under whiring arms holding blades, leeches, and chemicals.
