## Behind Blanket Road: Group of Seventeen (1)  
 Cyriaca verGrin 11/17/4/0  
 Spell: Invisibility  
 (Thinking engine staff 7/26/2/1)  
  
 8ft tall and perilously thin. A bathouse mistress. Single large bath with multiple secluded areas. Remains proudly ignorant of customers' business. Staff are Thinking Engines, 9 of them left, who can pass as entirely organic. Open all hours, staff rarely seen abroad, Cyriaca more so and only while accompanied by staff. 1 in 6 chance of a herbal bath day 1- Green, red berries, restores all Stamina 2- Blue with silver streaks, ensures a restful night's sleep 3-Red and twigs, smells like dirt, tightens skin drastically, so no one recognises you for a day 4-Green with green berries, relaxing, -5 Skill for the day 5-Hot mud, -2 Stamina +1 Skill for the day 6-Pink, smells like cake, calm & happy for the rest of the week, immune to fear and panic.  
  
  
   
## Ample Street: Further Adherence (2)  
Erumpet Catenary 4/6/2/0  
Skill: Poison  
  
Towers of tins of glues, gums and pastes. Popular solutions are sold by the barrell. Those spending more than a few minutes here may test their luck or else become lightheaded and start seeing things that are not there. Treat the hallucination by pulling out a pre-written adventure you have to hand and start running that loose and fast for 30 minutes or so. Once it ends you should interpret what their glue induced adventure achieved. This is an excellent way to push the characters into trouble or further adventure.  
  
  
## Ample Street: Houses of Snath (3)  
Bronze Requirement    Life Line    7/17/3/0    F5 (3)  
Bicor Chow    Flash (3)    5/7/3/0    F5 (3)      
Bicor, with a indented face so that his forehead reaches out as though to meet his chin, and Bronze, face round and squashed like the setting sun. They are the exclusive importers of the botanical houses of Snath, a far distant sphere whos people have mastered the engineering of plants for their uses. This pure white shop, clinical in its clenliness, houses glass boxes holding seeds nesting in cotton. The walls show their growth in various stages, eventualy turning in to a tall and beautiful quasi-sapient home. Purchase of a house comes with instruction on how to care for and train it once it is grown. Each house costs 100,000p.    
  
## Ample Street: The Farthest Moon (4)  
Vitalina Scream    Golden barge pilot    4/14/2/0    F5 (4)    Assayer of gold  
  
Vitalina's jumpsuit is festoned with ribbons, head to toe, in the fashion of a previous generation. Her trips through the spheres have placed her out of syncronicity with the city and driven her irreparably eccentric. Her office is full of daguerreotypes and watercolour recreations of the sights one could see if one commissioned her barge for a trip. She is eager for customers and enthusiastic about sky flight, but still involuntarily recoils and screams if touched. She feigns ignorance of the episodes and is grateful to those who play along.
