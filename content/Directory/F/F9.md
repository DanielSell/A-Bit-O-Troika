# F9  
## Northern Serene Wall Labyrinth Entrance (1)  
The entrance to the brown labyrinth and the ruins it towers over are the daytime home of the Ratchet Stab'ums, a saltime gamin gang. They will try to rob anyone who comes their way. Since they have decided that they would like to sign up to the clockwork legion they have become bolder and less scared of grievous injury in their activities, so even a group of well armed adventurors isn't safe. 1d6/PC gamins will try to extort money, sweets and alchohol from the party on threat of stabbing. The goblin guards watch on indifferently.
