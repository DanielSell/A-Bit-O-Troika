# G3  
## Tallow Street: The Fragrance of Spiritual Arbitration (1)  
Robust Amba    Fear    8/13/4/0    G3 (1)    engineer  
  
The nature of the smell changes day by day, but the street outside this office of arbitration always smells of deep, rich spice. The board of directors maintain that the first point of agreement between estranged parties begins with the nose, and once that beachead is established their expert arbiters can do the rest. Their services are expensive, and can take a while to begin since every arbitration's scent will need to be determined after extensive meetings with both sides to gather the nature and essence of he matter. Then, on top of that, they may need to source rare ingredients from outside of their extensive library of scents. All add cost and time. They have an impeccable track record, though will not deal with anosmic entities.  
  
  
## Ormon's Square: Third Advent Cottage (2)  
Luxorius Gilbert    Life Line    7/6/3/1(sheepskin coat)    G3 (2)    Steeplejack  
  
The building is festooned with ladders, bolted and secured at all heights along the walls and over the roof, giving it the appearance that it is built from them alone. Gilbert is inside, if the ladders are here, flat-cap on and smoking pungent roll-ups while working on his steam engines. The engines are a hobby, and if the ladders are gone then he is on a job somewhere in the vast city, half a world away up a condemned chimney dismantling it brick by brick with nothing but a sledge and a chisel.
