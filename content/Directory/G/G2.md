# G2  
  
## Ormon's Corner: Elmo's Fire (1)  
Bakery  
Elmo Grwst    7/12/2/0   
Torpor (3)     G2 (1)  
Sweet smelling smoke puffs out of the open windows of Elmo's home, drawing the rumbling attention of passing coaches and passengers. Elmo emerges, great bushy face and chefs smock flying, as he swings his tray of honeyed buns under peoples noses "only a penny! only a penny!".  
  
## Clatoyant Road: Besmearers (2)  
Lover Simon    5/9/2/1  
Sentry (1) G2 (2)    Plaisterers (plasterers)  
Filthy ladders bolted to the wall mark the workshop of a member of the plasterers guild. Lover Simon and his apprentices work fast and hard, stripping and plastering a house in a day no matter the cost. They descend on a wall like locusts, tearing across and leaving it clean and mottled, as is the fashion.  
  
  
## Ormon's Corner: The Burnt Pot  
Abated Daffodil    3/14/2/1(chainmail vest) (3)   
Healing  
  
Tinsmith Daffodil is long and lithe, like a shaved panther, and shuffles between the tin melting stations with the pots and pans he is fixing. His bizzarely small legs give him a jumbling gait and cause any customers nearby to get specked with molten tin, explaining Daffodil's spotted appearance which compliments his shape so well.
