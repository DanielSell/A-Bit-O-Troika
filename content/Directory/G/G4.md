## Ormon's Square: Most Ancient Fraternity of Crystal Navigators (1)   
Tatwine Madonna    Evaluate    5/2/2/0    G4 (1)    Guild master  
  
This gold and chrome building houses one of the oldest navigator academies in Troika. The Ancient Fraternity is not what it used to be, however, and makes its living by renting out the buildings that were once part of a sprawling decentralised university. Now only this building remains under their direct use, in which they train a token handful of students every year to maintain their charter licences and the rights to hide their extensive proprietary star charts. Education is free and by invitation only, the masters of the guild consult the charts and determine who would make the most valuable new student of the guild and extends them an invitation. Tatwine -- the youngest guild master the fraternity has ever nominated, at a sprightly 60 years old -- oversees their education personally and can often be seen greeting the students at the door in his gossamer space suit.  
  
## Behind Ripple Road: The Martyry of St Mungo (2)  
Cataldus    Banish Spirit (6)    3/6/2/1(leathery skin)    G4 (4)    Fan Makers  
  
An empty shrine with no priest which is nonetheless well cared for. Beggers gather here to share news and bring grievances from the across the neighbourhood. The minutes of which they provide to Cataldus, the fan maker. Cataldus is barely clothed, sunbeaten, and bent over, and is usually found in the Plaza of Time giving away fans made from the pages of the days newspaper. These disposable fans communicate to the beggers who see them being used, telling them of yesterdays business, of dangers coming, and word on the street.    
  
## Behind Ample Street: Golem & Harding Assurance Society  
Butterfly Principus    Earthquake (5)    7/2/2/3(cast iron articulated self-defence suit)    G4 (3)    Insurance broker  
  
For a small fee the Assurance Society will insure anyone's life against mishap. Getting them to pay requires more evidence than most can provide, and includes such things as alibi's from the beneficiaries, signed affidavits bound with curses against fraud (notarised and enforced by Belorgolax, Demon Prince of The Halls of Impalement), and the corpse of the policy holder, which they will insist on incinerating free of charge before payment is made.   
  
Current offers:  
General life insurance, valid in the city of Troika only: 124p/month, payout of 6,000p   
Travel Insurance: 500p/week with a multiplier based on the exact level on emanation from Troika you are travelling (or roll 1d6)  
  
## Behind Ripple Road: Mayme the Gremlin Genius (4)  
Stupid Mayme    Banish Spirit (6)    3/18/2/0    G4 (3)    Gremlin tamer  
  
This building was once a newspaper kiosk but has since been expanded upwards in a ramshackle scaffold. The noise is terrible, as if a bunch of drunks were arguing with a seabird at all hours of the day. Mayme, the owner and architect, has trained a gang of gremlins to ride tamed owls and deliver messages around the city. For a mere 10p she can deliver any single sheet of paper wherever you like, however she takes no responsibility for the message being eaten by the gremlin or the gremlin being eaten by the owl. Two things which happen maybe a quarter of the time.
