# G8  
## Behind Tun Road: Fine Dubbings  
Used clothes shop  
Endellion Friggins (5/12/3/1)  
Sword Fighting  
Catbold Friggins (7/11/3/1)  
  
Spell: Trueseeing  
  
Twin siblings watch over heaving piles of clothes heaped on ancient wooden tables and kept from falling and crushing customers only by their expert and inscrutable stacking system. The only clear space is the table on which the twins sit and take payment. They are happy to purchase old clothes but most of their stock is gathered at night from victims of the city. Searching through the clothes one may Test Their Luck to discover something unusual costing d666p. On failing there is a 1 in 6 chance the pile will collapse on you dealing damage as Club and causing the twins to dig you out while tutting.  
  
1- Brelade's Breeches: The old uniform of the alcalde's included and armoured pair of breeches, ensuring protection from knee to nipple. 1 Armour.  
2- Sleeping Cap: a result of the arts & crafts & sorcery movements unsuccessful industrialisation efforts. When placed on your head you must Test Your Luck or get a good nights sleep.  
3- Left Shoe of Midnight: A sandal with the capacity to attach to any foot short of an elephants and larger than a dog's. The shoe dances (beautifully) when any music is played, however a single shoe will likely result in the wearer being dragged about roughly while they try to learn to dance with their other foot.   
4- Eulalia's Scarf: Anyone seeing Eulialia's Scarff for the first time must test their luck or offer to buy it for d666p OR all of their money, whichever is less.  
5- Troikan Fighting Sock: an antique from the last revolution, this sock is reinforced so that any heavy object put in it will make it usable as a Flail without chance of it ripping.   
6- Brickmaker's Mittens: A pair of huge mittens connected by steel wire. They are broadly immune to heat and will protect your hands against anything that wouldn't also incinerate the wearer where they stood.
