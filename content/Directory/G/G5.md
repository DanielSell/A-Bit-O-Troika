# G5  
## St Edburgers Owl Hospital (1)   
Firmatus Adulation-War    Languages    4/18/4/0    G5 (1)    vet  
  
This charitable institution was founded out of the estate of the late explorer, philanthropist, and pretender to the throne of Troika, Baron Eustorgious. After leading a short lived revolution he entered into self exile among the crystal spheres aboard his golden barge, the *Forgiveness of Wisdom*. Locals suspect that he stipulated the construction and indefinite operation of an owl hospital in his will not because he loved owls, but because he hated Troika and wanted as many owls bothering people as possible.  
  
## 2 Ripple Road: Anhilation of The Possibility of Death  
Scrap Silver     7/4/2/0    Purple Lens (1)    G5 (2)        sword maker  
  
The Anhilation of The Possibility of Death -- or ‘The Possibility’ to locals and anyone who needs to go there more than once -- has enjoyed the benefits of being on such a busy road and capitalised on it by having famous window displays of finely dressed taxidermied animals fighting duels with their products across elaborate plaster dioramas. Once they were a primary sword maker for the Troika military, but since the war dried up they have been making most of their trade in collectable pencil sharpening devices.  
  
## 3 Ample Street: Diuma & Twine Phylactories  
Diuma Battle    7/9/2/0    Protection from Rain (1)    G5 (3)        cooper  
Alban Twine    7/16/3/0     Sleep G5 (3)    scribe  
  
Citizens looking to have relics or scriptures phylactorised know to come to Diuma & Twine for a speedy service. Kegs and roundelts fabricated while you wait, up and down to any size you wish, for any size of relic, hermetically sealed in a tarred and banded wooden receptical complete with custom fitted straps so you can better wear it on your person. They also sell a selection of pre-made phylactories from single rolled up lines of praise, all the way to complete torsos of -- admittedly lower prestige -- saints.  
  
## Ripple Road: Church of the Self-Immanent Subdivision (4)   
Primitive Luella    Affix (3)     4/7/2/4(Chosen by fate)    G5 (4)    Priest  
  
A heretical cult which doesn't recognise the saints of Troika, instead worshipping the city itself and its Universal Congress. As an act of devotion lay members maintain intricate maps of their neighbourhood, updating them daily to include everything from loft conversions to new paving stones. The maps, they say, contain sacred geometries from which they draw axioms and directions. Their messiah, Primitive Luella, can regularly be seen walking the streets barefoot and blindfolded, alone and oblivious to danger.  
  
## 5 Plaza of Time: Zohar Towers  
Jenaro Demetria    6/15/2/0     Purple Lens (1)    G5 (6)        tinsmith  
  
This stately home is tall, as its name suggests, and looms over its neighbours. What isn’t apparent however is that the entire building is a thin veneer, with no rooms being more than a couple of metres deep. At the centre of the house is a steel chimney whose origin is deep underground somewhere. It’s hard to walk through the house without going sidewards, and old Jenaro Demetria is incapable of doing it now without the help of one or more of his strapping serving boys. He lives alone, his wife having fell into the chimney decades ago and his children left and never came back soon after. His skin is pinched so he looks like some unseen hand has snatched the back of his head and is pulling with all its might, leaving him with a rictus grin at all times and a personality to match.  
  
Someone has been painting graffiti on the front door of Zohar “Woe to the wicked of this world, whose days are faulty and full of holes!”. It is re-applied within days of it being washed off by the help, who are considering leaving it on since their employer never leaves the house anyway and is unlikely to ever see it.  
  
  
## 6 Plaza of Time: Shelter 73  
Findan Plant    7/13/3/3    Babble    G5 (5)        Carpenter  
  
Findan Plant, retired detectorist, runs this tiny furniture repair shop on alternating Thirdays. The kiosk the shop occupies used to be the entrance to an underground shelter, the evidence of which can be seen in the storage basement which is a long wide staircase sliding away under the adjoining plaza and ending in a slimy pile of ancient rubble. Findan has built huge shelves  in order to make the stairs more agreeable to storing his wood scraps, but the damp makes them all curl in what he claims to be pleasing ways. He never gave up his detectorising ways and on alternating Thirdays he extends and shores up the shaft he is digging through the ancient rubble under his shop.  
  
  
## 7 Plaza of Time: The Deep-Sunken Intercourse  
Marius Craze    6/18/4/1    Drown (4)    G5 (7)        Fishmongers (sellers of fish and seafood)  
  
Marius runs his family business, the latest in a long line of Crazes to hawk fish in the Plaza. It all started with his seven times great grandpa who sold stolen fish heads to the various military camps which chose the Plaza as a beached to invade the city during his time. Now they own a grand building right next to the spot where that same grandpa died from artillery fire. But times aren’t good, and Marius is not the businessman his forbearers were, and he has resorted to desperate measures to maintain the family business. He has reconnected with the faith his forbears brought from the old country and has fashioned a effigy of fish heads in the basement which he sacrifices small mammals to. Sales are up, and seem directly related to the size and adorability of the sacrifice. Since this discovery Marius has been eyeing some of the slower gamins with a curious eye.
