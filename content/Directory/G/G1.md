## Clatoyant Road: The House of Casketshame (2)  
Unwelcome Casketshame 6/8/3/0      
Healing        G1 (2)    day laborer  
  
Old Casketshame lives alone, surrounded by sentimental gewgaws of a well lived life across the sky. He spends his time sitting outside his door reading or chatting to neighbours, and occasionally makes a bit of money helping out the local scavengers when they need some extra muscle.   
  
  
  
## Clatoyant Road: The Rosie House (3)  
Rosie Nemesius-Crispin        9/10/2/0      
Darksee G1 (3)    Cacogen  
  
If one knocks on the door of the Rodsie House it will open and shut behind you. Inside is bare except for a hole in the floorboards of the ground floor through which the face of Rosie is pressed, slightly proud of the floor. She cannot turn her head so you need to stand above her to ask your questions. She has intimate knowledge of the stars and what happens betwen them and she can be convinced to share with the appropriate gifts. These gifts change often but are always some kind of exotic flesh and obscure machines. Those who look to harm Rosie never leave the house.  
  
  
## Clatoyant roand and Ormons Corner: The Mule Toll  
Liberius Scab    Levitate    3/13/2/0    G1 (4)    mule driver  
Liberius saw an opportunity to profit at his own expense by turning the ground floor of his home into a small toll booth shortcut wetween Ormons Corner and Catoyant Road, cutting out the busy and frankly creepy traffic around the Serpentine Reserve. It is only large enough of single animal carts but it is always busy. Liberius sits upstairs and watches through a hole in the floor, ensuring mule drivers are throing a penny in the basket as they pass.
