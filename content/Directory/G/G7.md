# G7  
## Tun Road: Temple of All-Gods (1)  
Temple  
Bishop of Barley 9/16/3/0  
Spells: Natter, Cockroach, Fear, Read Entrails  
  
More like a hotel than a temple. A lobby with receptionist directing customers to the appropriate room of worship. 1 in 6 chance your god or gods is not represented. Open to requests of additions, which will be taken in to serious consideration by the Bishop upon a room becoming available. Bishop is young, willowy, dressed in a mishmash of holy vestments: mitres, furs, blood and berries etc. Willing and able to stand in for any holy man if one cannot be arranged. Has a chain full of holy symbols and is not above using it to batter miscreants in the street. Sub-basement caters to controversial murder cults, which he also administers on occasion.  
  
  
  
## Plaza of Time: Ripplegate Community Betterment Block (2)  
Pipenon Pebblesnarl    Gills    7/17/2/1    G7 (2)    Day laborer  
Ebba Hollowsheen    Thunder    8/11/4/0    G7 (2)    auctioneer  
  
Originally the treasure filled concretions which bubbled up from the Contus Crater were fought over and sold off right there at the sludge line, but more modern and civilised methods have prevailed in recent years. Now they are, by order of the Alcalde, claimed for the communal good and sold here to the highest bidder. The contents, if any, are impossible to guess (regardless of what ardent bidders might say) and so tempers regularly flare, requiring once sludge free-diver now porter, Pipenon to intervene. He's unasuming and getting on in life but he's confronted death in the dark too many times to be bothered by a few rowdy treasure speculators. Ebba, ex patrol alcalde and now state sponsored auctioneer, lets him do as he pleases and just tries to keep her jabber above the screams.  
  
  
## Tun Road: The Hardened Quiff  
Tailor  
Flavia Lawdog    Thunder (2)    6/10/4/0    G7 (3)    doublet maker  
  
A tailors made wealthy by its unique location, enabling it to service two cash rich customers who wish to spend their money before it's too late: Sludge divers and clockwork soldiers. They spend their money on outlandish fashions with architectural gold wire and stuffed animals, slashed backs and netted tights, Flavia does not judge their taste. Flavia is a head taller than most of her customers and hasn't aged a day by anyones estimate. She brushes off claims that she is an exultant hiding in plain sight and says it's just clean living and a commitment to her craft. Still, the careful observer might spot the slightest twitch when mention of the war is ever brought up.
