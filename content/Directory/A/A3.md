  
## Show Street, behind Demilune Road: The Warm Pits (1)  
Smith  
Circumstantial Clerus 7/12/2/3(metal armour advertising his skill) (1)  
Spell: Darkness (3)      
  
Clerus is a wet eyed, broadbacked and optimistic youth, having just founded his own smithey at the end of his appreticeship. Few customers stop, leaving him to scamper and flip in his advertisement armour trying to attract trade.  
  
## Demilune Road: Goban Woodmongers (2)  
Builders yard  
Villicus Goban    6/11/4/0  
Spell: Poison          
  
Surrounded by an army of labourers at all time, Villicus aproaches customers like a hungry cat. His face droops as though it was once stretched to several times larger than it is now, but he assures that it is a family trait. His grandmother stood in this very woodmongers with that same feature and sold fuel to half of Ripplegate.  
  
## Demilune Road: Lightmonger Station Mugwort (3)  
Lightmonger  
Equity Crucifixion    8/2/2/0      
Skill: Healing  
  
Closed during the day but as nonesong rolls by the shutters open and lightmongers travel forth on telescopic stilts to light the lamps for the night. Their tall hats they light as they leave which they use to ignote the posts, and its ever encroaching flame encourages speed in their route. Those looking to have new lights installed must speak to Equity, the regional manager. She sits like a doll at her desk in the loft, staring silently at maps and schedules and intelligence reports from her spies at the Public Works Comittee, all to better plan for tomorrow's shifts.  
  
## Demilune Road: Cider With Rose (4)  
Pub  
Agnes Harmony    6/2/1/0      
Spell: Protection from Rain   
  
Bulbs and lanterns and candles abound on every surface of this inn. The light is so intense that regulars have taken to wearing smoked glass spectacles to protect from the glare. Tourists invariably stop on their way through Ripplegate to visit the lights of Cider With Rose and see Agnes serve guests in her dress made of cut mirrors.  
  
## Demilune Road: The Secured Door (5)  
Nailmaker  
Hiltrude Nicephorus    6/6/3/0      
Spell: Animate (2)  
  
Hiltrude runs his workshop alone with the aid of great steaming machines of his devising. He marches through the swinging metal arms and pumping pistons deftly avoiding death as though it never crossed his mind as he tends his machines. At the end of it he has nails of acceptable quality in vast quantities, which he seems to be less excited about than the method of their devising. Customers agree that one day they will arrive to find him tangled in a flywheel, but until then they will continue to buy his daily supply.
