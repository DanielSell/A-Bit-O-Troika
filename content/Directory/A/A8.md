
## The Flushing Mouth (4)  
Reputationwax    Strength    9/5/4/0    B9 (3)    cymbal player  
A wide stone mouth has been beautifully made out of the brown tiles of the wall. Its lips stick out by a full foot or more and its lower jaw is invitingly low to the ground. The throat behind is a tiled tunnel sloping gently down. On a stool to the side sits goblin Reputationwax, dressed in a long grey gown with no leg holes, holding a single cracked cymbal. She sits comfortably in the philtrum of the mouth beneath a system of tin buckets which drip fetid water on her. She is soaked but seems to enjoy the sound the water makes on her instrument. Reuptationwax says she is a musician and offers to perform her rain-drip-cymbal pieces, all of which last for hours. The hole, she says, leads to the goblin hoard and she's not supposed to let anyone pass, but no one has come here in so long. In actual fact the hole leads to a sewer beneath the Plaza of Time (H6), when the party clime in is is wide and gently sloping, but it soon gets steeper. When it gets almost too steep Reputationwax will flush all her water buckets down the hole and cause the party to either test their luck orslide all the way to the plaza. Test them in reverse marching order, and anyone who fails takes out everyone in front of them automatically. Getting back up the hole would be very difficult.
