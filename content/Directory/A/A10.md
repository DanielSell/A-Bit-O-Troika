# C1  
## Tallow Street: Leadeater's Gold Pound (1)  
A burnt out gold beater's workshop, now occupied by a gang of gamins who decorate their teeth with the shreds of gold left behind in the ashes and war relentlessly with the yellow scarves and gloves.  
  
## Tallow Street: Cockshut's (2)  
This shop, once selling imported eartenware, is recently abandoned. Through the shttered and boarded window one can spy the probable cause, whereby shelves and shelves of goods appear to have shattered in place and sit there still, left in disgust.   
  
## Tallow Street: Once More, Swine (3)  
The old tanners was relocated out of the neighbourhood after the fire. The building is now inconsistently occupied by children who alternate smashing things and inexpertly tanning hard leather scraps to stuff under their clothes.  
  
## Tallow Street: The Once and Future Spring (4)  
This matress maker took the fire as a sign to retire and shut up shop. The small building is empty and clean, with only the sign to suggest its past.  
  
## Tallow Street: The Fortunate Everyday (5)  
This general good store has been open and unmanned for as long as anyone can remember. The sundry items are always fresh and restocked and customers juditiously place the correct money for their purches behind the counter in an ever growing pile. No one has had the nerve to steal anything, it hasn't even crossed their minds.
