
## Ephor Way: The Burkhouse  (1)
Firehouse  
Fireservant Medrald Right (6/5/4/0) (1)  
Spell: Assume Shape  
  
A fire house, whos staff employ huge expertled hurled nets in the snuffing of untamed fires. The essential service they perform masks the fact, though it is no secret, that they are a cult of fire worshipers. Inside is blackend stone kept in total darkness, where the disciples of the temple wait in meditation for their next audience with god.  
  
## Ephor Way: Dankess Bottled (2)  
Pub  
Bonfilius Aloysius 6/16/3/3(exotic metals stiched into clothes)  
Spell: Presence      
Copper Beard 5/11/2/2(divine grace)  
Skill: Fly  
  
Once a famous explorer, Bonfilius has retired to the comparatively peaceful occupation of brewing ale using a recipe he brought back from a distant world. The black, frothy drink is foul but undeniably morish, a tension which has sparked the imagination of customers across the city. Lately he has been accompanied everywhere by a scroll and stylus wielding cherubim named Copper Beard, who claims to be sent here to record Bonfilius' eventful life for posterity. The attention is unwanted but no amount of shooing will get Copper Beard to leave and thus he is resigned.   
  
## Behind Ephor Way: The Reluctable Yellow (3)  
Dyer  
Incinerated Jakob 5/11/2/0       
Spell: Grow (2)        A2 (3)    Clothworkers  
  
The windows are painted over and the doors are all tightly locked, the only clue to the operation taking place withing is the colourful smoke which rises from the chimey stacks. Jakob developed a new kind of yellow cloth, more brilliant than any other, and he hides its recipe closely. All people know is that the unrefined dye is intensely caustic if mishandled, leaving many of the staff gruesomely scarred. Children collect the vibrant yellow dust that settles on their rooftops, and compress it into slingshot pellets with which to wage their internecine wars.  
  
## Ephor Way: The Sovereign Sun Society (4)  
Bank  
Dolston Tribute 8/13/2/4(hermetically sealed armour with an independent air supply, of ancient design)  
Skill: Sword Fighting  
  
The Sovereign Sun Society is a private bank operating on behalf of local businesses. Their founding is relatively recent, spurred on by the accidental excavation of an ancient subterranean vault. Those wishing to deposit or withdraw money approach Dolston, the battle hardened bank teller, who will assign an adventurer to delve the monster-riddled depths of the vault on your behalf. The whole operation is funded by incidental wealth discovered in the largely unmapped vaults by the freelance adventurers. The turnover is quite high, so new staff are always welcome.
