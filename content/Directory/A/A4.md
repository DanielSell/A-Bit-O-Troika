 
## Demilune Road: The Skin On Your Back (1)  
Tannery  
Wasp Maurus 8/4/1/2      
Spell: Ironhand (3)  
  
The first thing one notices about this tannery is the smell, or lack thereof; Wasp Maurus has gone to great lengths to ensure his superating pits of flesh smell sweet to his neighbours. His acute talent for perfumery has become so famous that people now come more to purchase bottles of his deoderising perfumes than his skins.  
  
## Chara Street: Keck and Keek (2)  
Pawnbrokers  
Beuno Schotin Panemotis 7/7/3/0      
Grow        A4 (2)    Banker  
Beuno sits by the door of his pawn brokers and won't move for anything. His voluminous chintzy suit matches the ragged chintzy chair he sinks in and gives him the illusion of being a head floating in flowers.  His only purpose is to take money, call out prices, and shrug at questions of use or provenance. Anyone looking to sell an item is instructed to slide them inside the slot at the back of the shop and wait for the money to come back the same way. Questions about the system to Beuno will be met with his characteristic upholstered shrug. Thieves will be blasted with a pistolet from within the folds of Beuno's pile, but never chased.   
  
If a character has had something stolen from them recently they may test their luck to find it here for sale.  
  
## Chara Street: The Bright Polder (3)  
Glazier and painter of glass  
Joan-Harold    4/4/3/1  
Trapping        A4 (3)    Glazier and painter of glass  
  
This workshop has a heady mixture of temple smells: oils, smoke and perfumes cloud the senses of visitors, while Joan-Harold wisely chooses to wear a damp rag around her face. She will take commissions from all faiths and prides herself on her ability to accomodate most ritual requirememnts for sacramental glass. The only line she has had to enforce so far is the sacrifice of thinking things inside the shop.  
  
## Hypotherm Circus: The Lasslorn Lama   
Wine seller  
Mean Anne    Farseeing    8/9/4/0    A4 (4)    Glass-maker  
Grand Wonder Verulus    earthquake    9/9/1/0    A4 (4)    Wine seller  
  
Tourists and wealthy residents alike come to drink the rebottled swill of the Lasslorn Lama. Verulus, a predatory creature of easy graces and barely hidden spite, prowls the outer spheres pretending to be a god or mythical creature descending from the heavens on his golden barge, requiring wine or other novel fermented juices to take back with him. On his return Mean Anne fabricates elabortate glass bottles which perfectly capture the backwards flavour of the people who produced the wine inside. All are extraoridinarily expensive, mostly quite poor, and occasionally outright poisonous. But you're paying for the story, as they say.
