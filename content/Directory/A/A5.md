
## Hypotherm Circus: Health Guttatim (1)  
Autumnal Lie    8/17/2/1    Ride    A5 (1)        Barbers (including surgeons and dentists)  
  
Anyone who can demonstrate their skill with a razor and a trepane can rent a barbers chair from Autumnal Lie. Lie, the owner of the barber surgery, is a huge man, thickly hairy and constantly crouching away from ceiling beams. Despite his huge fingers he cuts and shaves better than most and his broad laughter and excellent gossip endeers him to everyone. Prices vary: Dry haircut, 12p; Bloodletting 10p; Teeth extraction 15p; Minor wounds 20p; Amputation 50p; Cupping 15p; wet haircut 20p; trepenation 35p; wet shave 10p. Other procedures may be requested and granted if someone is on hand who is able.   
  
## Behind Hypotherm Circus: Wurzelwinks Soft Furnishings (2)  
Toros Aqua    Disguise    6/18/2/1    A5 (2)    Upholders (upholsterers)  
  
Entering this large building deposits you into a room the size of a caravan with several catalogues dangling from ropes. The catalogues are full of poor illustrations of chairs, often repeated, with no prices or descriptions other than numbered items and fabric designs. One takes their selection up to the desk and provides the clerk their number, after which they will disapeer for ten or twenty minutes and return to tell you the design is out of stock and no they cannot tell you exactly what is availible that would take too long. Eventually you will leave. The building is in fact a safe house and break room for field operatives of the Poplation Adjustment Bureau. Active agents know the correct item and design to request to be waved through to the back where they'll find a fully equipped and well defended place to lay low for as long as needed after a long day's work. Toros Aqua is the nondescript matron of the house and ensures the curious public come to sticky ends before they poke too hard. Anyone probing the building will, in order, be brutally mugged by a gang of gamins, have a roof tile fall on their head, have their residence burn down, and, if all else fails, be shot by a rogue crossbow bolt through a window.  
  
## Stele Mews: The House Antoninus (3)  
Sir Severus Test Antoninus    diminish    3/18/3/0    A5 (3)    Knight  
This residence is tall and fortified, clearly the turret of some old wall that was at some point roughly cut away. A blue plaque on the door designates this building as being seperate from the city of Troika, unceeded territory of the Moth Kings, residence of Sword of the Stake, Master of Arms, First General, Sir Severus Test Antoninus. Sir Antoninus himself is very old and doesn't take the whole thing too seriously. He took the position from his father and his children have no interest in becoming a tourist attraction and so have denounced it, meaning the territory will default to the city before long. Sir Antoninus' enthusism has been rising recently since young people interested in the Moth monarchy have been spending a lot of time here. They bring strong tea and hard biscuits and talk about the good old days until long into lauds.   
  
## Myste Road: The Cannonhole (4)  
Domninus Rhais    Natter (1)    8/13/2/0    A5 (4)    cloth seller  
  
This building is no building at all, rather a chunk of old fortification wall from the distant past. A huge chunk of it has been carved out and inside Domninus operates his fabric stand. Pinned to the walls all along this wall are gaudily dyed squares of fabric suitable, he claims, for table cloths, blankets, shawls, wall decoration, and anything else he can think of off the top of his head. The designs are universally tacky, unfashionable, and weirdly popular with tourists.  
  
## Myste Road: The Crab Kitchen  
Disgusting Hugh    5/5/2/2    Ironhand (3)    A5 (5)        Cooks  
Hugh stands alone in a sea of superating pots and pans. He works wordlessly because he has no mouth, he whole head is a mass of chitenous legs and eyes and he sees nothing wrong with that. Everyone who comes in he offers food with no need of compensation, but few accept. His concept of palatable cuisine is out of sync with his customers biology, so 1 in 10 of his dishes is intensely, though inadvertently, poisonous. The alley behnd his kitchen is filled with rotting food.  
  
## Behind Chara Street: Logs  
Ludmilla Mirthful    5/7/2/3    Exchange Shape (5)    A5 (6)        sawer of wood  
  
Ludmilla's long hair flies wildly as she saws. In an extatic spasm she renders wood down to useful sizes for customers. They joke that she would do it for free if she didn't work here, and they're not wrong. When there is no work she sits, blank eyed, waiting. At night she goes home only reluctantly.
