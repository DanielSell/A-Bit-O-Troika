
## Myste Road: The Continuous Sacrifice (1)  
Donat Mud    Babble    6/14/4/0    A6 (1)    Silke seller  
  
Donat, the silk seller, is fingelress and proves the worth of his wares by his inability to stop their flowing magnificence from getting away from him. A demonstration of one of his patternd silks includes a fountain of cascading cloth thrown high with energetic movements. Any question of spider silk will be met with a stern request to leave the store, but later in the day an urchin will approach with an address and a time. There is a 5 in 6 chance the note leads you to an agent of Donat selling spider silk thread and cloth, and a 1 in 6 chance it is an alcalde/spider-bank joint sting operation.

## Myste Road: St Erkenwald's Horse Sanctuary (3)  
Sodon Leobard    Ember (2)    6/2/1/0 (double evrything on horseback)    A6 (3)    Farriers (horseshoe makers and horse veterinarians)  
  
Old Sergeant Sodon of the CMXIII cataphracts oversees this haven for horses. People come to him for horse shoeing and medical care of all descriptions since he is the cheapest in town. While Sergeant Sodon is a flint hearted thug but he loves horses with every fibre of his being and doesn't think much of other less commited horse workers and would rather take every job and ensure the health and happiness of his animals. Anyone unable to pay for his services will have their horse confiscated by Sodon and added to his semi-wild horse herd which roams the nearby streets. Gods help anyone who harms or steals them, for soon the will be visited by a heavily armed cataphract on a steaming stallion.   
  
## Blanket Road: Wenod Can't Go Home (4)  
Wenog Juice     Read Stars (1)    3/15/3/0    A6 (4)    Goblin and spice merchant  
  
Wenog is a fugitive of the Goblin King's justice. He was once a spice trader, importing bitter ash from the Great Wastes into the heart of a the labyrinth. He became very welathy and was generous with it. He never cheated anyone, nor took more than his due, and he built goblin schools where young goblets could learn numbers. Public outrage at his wanton un-Goblin-like behavior became so great that the Goblin King himself decreed that Wenog should be thrown out the nearest window immediately. Luckily for Wenog he had invested in tempered double glazing in all his schools, so the king's guards settled for throwing him out the door instead. He left with whatever he could carry and opened a spice shop in Troika at the very edge of the labyrinth. He spends most of his time wistfully staring out of his (untempered) shop window at the brown walls of his old home.  
  
## Blanket Road: The Mighty Tun (5)  
Monica Lydwine    5/10/2/3    Grow (2)    A6 (5)        cooper  
  
A noisey cooperage utilising black iron machines of goblin design. Lumber is unloaded in the side yard and processed into firkns, tuns, and hogsheads through a many layed, smoke belching, arm tearing mess of machines. Monica, the overseer, nervusly tugs her plated overalls around her like a cacoon, terrified that she might be hooked and dragged into a machine at any moment. She directs the workers feeding into and out of the machines via a megaphone at a safe distance. Anyone entering the factory must test their luck or be chewed up or struck by a machine, dealing damage as large beast.
