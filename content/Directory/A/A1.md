## Ephor Way: The Ironwork (1)  
Burnt out, it's vast cauldrons of iron burst weeks ago leaving its front wall gone and in its place a glacier of iron partially blocking the street. Road workers stand by looking tired and disinterested while street urchins climb and push each other off the piles of burnt timber and iron.  
  
## Ephor Way: Goilson's Patibulary (2)  
This dancing school has been eaten in to by the previously molten iron of the ironworks across the street. A neat chunk of the building has collapsed where the iron now sits, and all around is burnt black. Abandoned and sorely missed.  
  
## Ephor Way: The Decollated Man (3)  
The headless figure who served as the sign of this pub now stands somewhat deliberately in the middle of the collapsed and blackened skeleton of the building. THe local children have taken to carving the names of teachers and parents that over step their authority in the body of the man.
