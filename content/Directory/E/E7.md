# E7   
## The Brown Labyrinth Entrance on Box Market: A criminal pleads his case (1)  
Thief Chunk    Healing    7/14/2/1    B7 (3)    duelist  
  
Chunk is worse for wear. His dashing fabrics are soiled and torn, his tights are laddered on his emaciated legs are dangling out of the gibbet. Troikan traders passing through the gate toss up the occasional scrap or bottle to keep him sustained, but he would quite like to go home. While on the labyrinth side of the gate the alcalde have no jurisdiction and so Chunk remains. He has long given up on claiming innocense and now loudly exclaims how he knows how to get into the goblin hoard and he'll share the secret with anyone who gets him out of here. He is watched at all times by four goblin guards on gate duty.  
  
  
## The Brown Labyrinth Entrance Off Box Market: A dangerous scaffold (2)  
Waste Conwall    Flash (3)    8/11/2/0    E7 (2)    Framework Knitters  
  
Repairs are being performed on the gates. Goblins have erected huge scaffolds which sway worryingly as they nonchalantly clamber about fixing things. The guards at the gate are nowhere to be seen, and no traders or locals pass in or out. Children, however, are watching from the other side of the market. Anyone passing through here must Test their Luck or be hit by a falling tool, piece of masonry, or goblin, receiving damaged as Modest Beast.  
  
## Blanket Road: A Plain and Unassuming Tenement (3)  
Albina Spring    5/6/1/0    Sentry (1) E7 (3) Bone Worker  
  
The unamed building slouches, threatening to slide itself on to of the makret traders outside at any moment. Its raggedy state is responsible for the htinner end of the market row, most sellers not willing to be crushed for the sake of a cheap pitch. The single remaining resident is Ms. Spring, who walks the alleys at night in a gossamer nightgown looking for bones. These she takes home and fashions into attractive charms and decorations which she sells out of the one working window on market day. Scores of unsolved crimes end up dragged back here and stored as future raw materials.  
  
## Behind Blanket Road: Box Market Depot (4)  
Odulf Sand    Ride    8/9/1/1    E7 (4)    Flag-stone worker  
  
By night market traders store their carts and prepare their goods here. Odulf, ruddy with youth, fixes wheels, patches awnings, shoes beasts, and any other small task the traders might have. He works entirely on their good will and generosity, which the box market traders are not known for, but for Odulf they make an exception. He lives in a barrow parked in the back of the hall, donated to him by a retired cabbage merchant, and is widely loved and known for being affable, strong as a donkey, and easily fooled.  
  
## Behind Blanket Road: Second Sight Optometry (5)  
Mysterious Coalition    6/16/2/1    Jolt (1) Weaver  
Mysterious Coalition, a three eyed ogre, has operated this optometrist for a few months. He came from a poor family and paid his way into an optometry apprenticeship in which he ate his master and assumed his position. No one has argued. He tests patients eyesight with paintings of gruesome horrors, each more horrifying than the last. He commissions his tests from local artists, of whom he is a great patron.
