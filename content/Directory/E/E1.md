# E1  
## Comfit Street: Fontodery Court (1)  
Judge Frilly Pig 7/14/2/0      
Spell: Illusion  
  
Bailiff Simplicity 5/18/1/1  
Spell: Affix (3)   
  
Frilly operates a small court house specialising in exotic jurisdictions. Signs in the window boldly claim that no matter where you are from (outside of Troika) Frilly Pig can provide a court and a verdict in your native mode of justice with full authority or your money back. The corridors up to the public galleries (1p admision) are plastered floor to ceiling in certificates, grants, diplomas, blood pacts, and vows from uncountable places.  
  
## Comfit Street: The Incredible Melting Man (2)  
Hallvard Valerian 5/5/1/0      
Spell: Earthquake (5)  
  
Hallvard sits at the middle of his shop looking like a pile of rags and bones. He casually reaches his long spindly arms over the heads of his customers to reach for examples of his good works hanging from the rafters like a mass of gallows. People come from all over the city to sit for an imago of themselves, or to buy effigies and novelty candles made in the image of popular onging or past cases across the street in the courthouse.  
  
## Comfit Street: The Three Parted Jar (3)  
Whirling Virginia    5/8/2/0      
Skill: Second Sight      
  
The floor of the shop is a mix of fine dust and shards of pottery. Virginia is tall, cricket-like in aspect, her skin spackled red and blue and always with fine clothes to match as though camoflagued for an alien world. Customers bring their old erthenware here to sell or be assessed, though time wasters and hagglers will see their goods hurled at the door from Virginia's pendulous arms.   
  
## Familiarity Way: Dreich House (4)  
Sick Ritbert 5/11/2/0      
Hurricane (5)      
  
Oven Stoker Anne-Francoise Boyish 3/3/2/0      
Levitate      
  
Sick Ritbart is a heaving egg of a man, large round spackled and cracked, who every morning receives his orders slipped under the door by the shadowey Public Works Committe for whom he digs up and lays down street paving. Ritbert and Anne-Francoise have split this concrete cottage down the middle. Both pretend the other doesn't exist, even though Ritbert must awkwardly climb through the window every morning to go to work. No amount of convincing will get them to acknowledge the situation.   
  
## Comfit Street: The Laughing Tap (5)  
Intense Portianus    3/17/2/1      
Affix (3)   
This bland building has a single tap on the outside with a coin slot next to it. Putting pennies in will cause the tap to run whatever concoction Portianus has hosed up today into your waiting mug. The people nearby, maybe unconcious, wailing, laughing or fighting, can suggest the effect of the brew today, but Portianus isn't telling and doesn't let in visitors.  
  
1, Thick nourishing brew, healing as a provision  
2, Electric psychic storm, inflicting an Oops roll on drinkers  
3, Deadly pale ale which causes momentary death. Careful where you fall.  
4, Fight juice, causing the imbiber to become hyper aggressive and ignore all damage until the next morning, when they receive all of it  
5, Delicious sour brew, causing those drinking it to moan in disgust at the flavour and then immediately go back for more  
6, "Just" water. No noticable effect.  
  
## Comfit Street: The Perfumed Awl (6)  
Master Aching Pattern 5/2/2/0      
Spell: Assume Shape (4)  
  
Above the builders yard and workshops swings Aching Pattern in a suspended harness of his own design granting him neutral boyancy in the air. From above he shouts orders and throws bolts at indifferent staff, only coming to the ground when a customer shows. All construction work undertaken, no job is too small for the crew of the Perfumed Awl.
