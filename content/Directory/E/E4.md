## Ironspike Lane: Cuboidal Roses (1)  
Tressle Vinegood 10/20/4/0  
Spell: Coal Resolve

## Ironspike Lane: The Eternal Mover (3)  
Champion Comgall    Open (1)    7/3/4/1    E4 (1)    tailor  
  
The crown glass windows distort the maninkins behind it into lurching headless ghouls, kept away from the viewer by leaded bars. Once inside they settle down, and are clothed in the characteristically tightly fitting togas of Mr Comgall, master tailor. He is a small man, wrapped and bound always in his latest designs, cheerfully squeeking as he moves through both his tight leather outfit and his tones of excitement from between his tightly drawn lips. He is secretly a parchment witch, stitched together from exquisite leather so well treated as to be totally convincing. His clothes cover the bulk of his body where he does not bother to attach flesh.  
  
## Ironspike Lane: Mock For Mock (4)  
Pearl Chain    6/17/2/1    Awareness    E4 (4)        hunter  
  
The smell wafts out the open shutters on the front of this warehouse. Acrid, stinging chemicals mixed with blood. The inside is very clean, the tiled walls and floor are hosed down before a customer can see any grisliness. Animals abound in various states of preparation for stuffing and mounting by Pearl and their assistants, who remain in a constant low buzz of motion. Pearl keeps an alzabo in the attic to which they fed the remains of their departed husband, whom they visit and speak with every day. The beast shows genuine affection for Pearl, who knows that, if given the chance, it would eat them and escape. This doesnt bother them as being together forever in the psyche of the alzabo, so Pearl's logic goes, is the next best thing. The staff are aware of the situation but know better than to comment.

Hubert is newly graduated from an apprenticeship with Champion Comgall (E4 (3)), with whom he maintains the warmest relations. He has applied his masters skills in skin tight materials to magnificent handwear. Gloves have not been in mode for an age, but Hubert is betting on them coming back big once his custom shrunk leather driving gloves start circulating at the popular parlours. Any day now.
