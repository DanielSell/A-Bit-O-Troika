## Comfit Street: Rubber Tree Monkey (3)  
Calimerius    3/8/2/1  
Presence (1)        A10 (3)    shoemaker  
  
Customers book months in advance to be fitted for one of dead-eyed Calimerius' rubber shoes. The process requires manhandling, moulding, cutting, and laquering  all while the customers feet are firmly bound in one of the shops casts. The day's customers take the opportunity to chat to each other across the room while Calimerius works on their feet.

## Familiarity Way: United Workers Yard (5)  
Malign Alfred 9/18/2/2      
Light (1)  
  
Alfred worked as apprentice builder for The Perfumed Awl on Comfit Street and developed, along with his fellow staff, a severe dislike for Master Aching Pattern. After coming together to complain to the master builder they were laughed at and pelted with bolts until they left. In an act of spite a few staff pooled their lifes hoards to start a competing buidlers yard directly behind their old masters'. Soon after its founding Malign Alfred lived up to his name, cutting his fellow workers out of the business and replacing them with maleable young laborers. Aching Pattern is almost proud.
