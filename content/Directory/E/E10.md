Once a pub but long ago demolished to make way for the Brown Labyrinth, now all that remains is a single wall holding fragments of floors, peaked with a jolly simian grotesque. Fell Timothy leads a small but energetic cult of Saint Hugh from what was once the attic but is now little more than a precarious ledge. An image of the saint appears each night upon the wall in steaming pitch, which he interprets with the help of his most loyal disciples, Epimachus and Cobra. Every Tuesday Fell emerges from the flapping tarpaulins of his chapel covered in burns and stuck-on scraps of paper and shouts down the weeks message to gathered adherents, pilgrims and looky-loos, which usually takes the form of a I DONT KNOW YET BUT IT SHOULD BE SOME UNDERCOVER POPULATION ADJUSTER SHIT
