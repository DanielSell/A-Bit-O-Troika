# E5  
## Ample Street: The Quartered Snake (1)  
Healthcare  
Dr. Revered Violator    8/17/2/0    Shatter (3)    E5 (1)          
  
A large shop with an overwhelming chemical stench that one feels from the back of their throat to the top of their head which triggers hallucinations of blue fire after too long. Violator sits neatly at a counter by the door, ready to take requests. "I want to know what it feels like to be an owl", one might say, and so he walks off into the maze of jars full of creatures, plants and objects stored in jars of liquid to find an owl he thinks would most suit the customer whereupon he returns and unceremoniously flicks the liquid in their face. The descent into whatever experience you paid for starts when you sleep and continues until you wake up. Prices vary depending on the rarity or danger of the experience, with a cat or and owl costing 50p, all the way up to demon nobility or specific people costing tens of thousands of pence.  
  
## Hypotherm Circus: The Hilding Place (2)  
Needlemaker  
Servan Constructille    Brittle Twigs (2)    3/4/2/0    E5 (2)    Needlemakers  
Servan is a bug eyed creature with the bearing of a mantis creeping up on it's prey. He perches over the skins of unrecognisable animals, plucking the sharp quills from it with swift actions before carving them down with a stubby knife grasped in the thumb. His needles are fine, strong and expensive, starting at 1p each and going up to 60p for a pair of spined knitting needles.  
  
## Ample Street: Hope & Hope & Co. (3)  
Surveyors  
Revolting Salo    7/8/2/1    Purple Lens (1)    E5 (3)  
  
Salo sits half naked in the shattered mess of this huge office, the last member of the firm. He wanders about holding conversations with long gone secretaries, attends imagined meetings, and goes home at the end of a long day to sleep in the Plaza of Time. The firm is, however, still operational and has a reputation for being wildly optimistic and lenient with its assessments, making it popular amongst unsavory sorts.  
  
  
  
## Behind Hypotherm Circus: Mineral Light (4)  
Scrubbed Nereus    5/4/4/0    Darksee (1)    E5 (4)        goldsmith  
  
The dazzling interior in tiled in gold, everything is plated or painted to be bright as the sun. The representative of the Moth Kings, Nereus, sits in the corner, too large to stand up, an ogre whose brain has been replace with impecable golden clockwork clearly sprouting from the back of his skull. Through decades old peace treaty the Moth Kings must maintain one place whereby the citizenry of Troika may buy their hair-fine golden crowns, and by spite they offer only one every month sat on a pedastle here, fiercly fought over under the watchful eye of the clockwork minded ogre.  
  
## Between Ample Street and Blanket Road: Selyf's Jarble (5)  
Selyf Saturnius    7/6/2/4    Evaluate kiln maker  
  
An open workshop with apprentices busily assembling portable kilns from river clay and yellow-black Troikan brick. At the centre, piping sweet smoke, is the entirely sealed edifice of Selyf Saturnis, the kiln master. His apprentices wheel him from place to place so he can inspect their work. They put their ear to his scorching hot brick to hear is advice coming from deep within.
