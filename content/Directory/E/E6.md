## Behind Blanket Road: O You Dancers Hall (1)  
Winifred Cordian    Thunder (2)    8/12/2/1(leathery skin)    E6 (1)    stilt dancer  
  
Palu Achatius    Skeletal Counsel (3)     8/16/2/0    E6 (4)    stilt dancer  
  
Sullen Theodric    Starry Orb (4)    7/5/3/0    E6 (4)    stilt dancer  
  
The O You troupe practises the traditional stilt dance of Troika city fetes. They tie stilts to their legs and perform mock battles dressed in costumes in the shape of towering giants with great big mouths and cartoonishly large hands. The biggest costumes require multiple dancers all on top of each other, operating different parts of the costume. The troupe is in the midst of a schism since the last troupe leader died. Winifred lead the troupe as de facto leader through the final years of the previous head's decrepitude and doesn't see why that should change now. Palu cared for their leader on his deathbed and claims the head was left to him in the leader's final words. And Sullen Theodric has simply absconded with the leader's costume, only returning with it to lead the celebrations on fete days knowing full well none of the troupe would dare man-handle him out of the suit for fear of damaging it. Any one of them would greatly appreciate some outside arbitration.  
  
  
## Ms. Flannans School for Disobedient Children (2)  
Domination Town    Earthquake, Etiquette    8/17/4/4(impervious undead flesh)    E6 (2)    Educator  
  
A red brick school with a reputation even the gamin gangs respect. Hollow eyed children silently shuffle in and out under the supervision of black clad school mistresses who wouldn't look out of place frozen to death half way up a mountain. The current head mistress is Ms. Town, a disarmingly agreeable person who the children nonetheless give the widest berth. Her policy is one of empathy and accessible fees for all parents, and cast iron discipline for their children. The school has a near perfect track record of reform.  
  
## Pharisatha's Exit (3)  
Conus Abatement Butter    Purple Lens (1)    6/17/3/1(stab vest)    E6 (3)    Pawnbroker  
  
Conus's head hangs in a sling on a series of rails and pulleys, his neck snakes back to his body, sat at a desk and tugging on ropes to move his head around the shop. He specialises in clearing and selling the possessions of the recently deceased, and is very good at it. He maintains a list of the sick and the elderly and regularly sends his employees around to check in on them, have a cup of tea, maybe tidy up a bit, all while taking a sneaky inventory. It's an open secret, but both sides maintain the facade, and if a neglectful family member suddenly starts sniffing around the house-clearers are no slouches in a tussle.
