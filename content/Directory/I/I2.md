## Ripple Road: Orange Joy Coming Soon (1)  
Orange importers  
Abandoned?  
Previously the Devious & Devious Banking Association, it was aquired by the current owners after a hostile takeover. Why the orange importers wanted and were able to do so is a mystery, but the Serpentine Reserve hasn't moved to stop them so everyone just assumes it is fine and dandy. The building, once brightly tiled, has been roughly painted in blues and orange by the new owners. Signs expressing the health benefits of 'orange fruit' appear on shuttered windows and doors and are changed frequently without anyone seeing by who. Members of the previous bank have resorted to pushing letters through the cracks in the boarded windows with their requests, all of which are answered with sweet smelling parcels of documents or cash, along with fresh orange fruit.

## Ripple Road: Harshness Fine Imported Homes (3)  
Harshness Respectatus    Languages (1)    5/2/2/1    I2 (3)    Farmers  
  
  
An airlock of thick leather curtains lead into a red-lit interior. The shop has a series of showrooms with miniatures of organic looking houses wedged into a bland and angular city. Harshness, frail, withered, and carried around by a transparent thinking engine, appears the moment anyone thinks of a question. These houses are for sale, he'll say, and all you need to buy is one small seed. For 90,000p you can take a seed the size of a snooker ball and plant it in a sunny spot. With a little water it will grow into a marvelous two up and two down home, perfectly snug to its environment with working plumbing, doors, and a home security of strangling vines which responds to its owner.  
  
## Tallow Street: The Salt Depot (4)  
Frumentius Despair  
9/6/3/0  
Skill: Jolt          
Occupation: Salter, trader of salts and chemicals   
  
THe street outside this warehouse is stained and worn by the chemical off-spills of arriving carts. The workers come out to meet the arriving shipments clad from head to toe in vulcanised suits with breathing tubes trailing back into the interior. Frumentius walks among them and the arriving barrels in nothing but his day clothes, inspecting labels and sampling lids with his ready crowbar. People gossip that he may be a thinking engine, to explain his indifference to the daily risk of a mortal scolding or dissolution. The gossip has reached him and in recent months he has taken to wearing a cloth over his mouth during the busier parts of the day.
