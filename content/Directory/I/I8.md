## Behind Graisle Street: Tintin-Athor House (1)  
Creamy Stephen 5/3/2/0     Banish Spirit (1) Knight  
  
Slouching in the back alleys of Graisle Street is Tintin-Athor House, built entirely from rich, blue-glazed bricks mortared with thick veins of pitch. The elastic mortar causes the structure to warp and undulate over the year and yet it somehow never cracks or crumbles. Sir Stephen, who built the house with a great fortune in bearer bonds brought home from the war, still lives here in the attic with his collection of unusual birds. He leaves the window open and they come and go freely, often terrorising the neighbourhood or immediately escaping when he acquires them. He is always in need of replacements. The rest of the house is given over to the embroidery circle of his late wife, which has grown into a political force in local politics where even the mighty worry at being the topic of their idle chatter.

## Graisle Street: Nori & The Noah Theatre (3)  
Ed Keg     5/3/2/0    Farseeing (2)     I8 (3)        Puppeteer  
  
The tiny frontage of this theatre is cocooned in red and yellow cloth, regularly replaced so it always looks fresh. It is occupied by the Nori & Noah puppet troupe, an ancient lineage of puppeteers claiming an origin before the current city of Troika existed. No one knows what to make of that but they do know better than to argue with a professor with a swazzle. The theatre itself is huge and honeycombed with intimate booths suitable for fifty people at a squeeze. In each is a stage with an episodic, violent and comedic drama being played out with glove puppets. The shows never end, all hour and all days, with audiences coming and going as they please. Ed Keg is the current Head Professor and de facto leader of the theatre, his huge arched bulk fills the narrow corridors of his theatre, and when he dons the puppets you can tell it's him by their bulging seams.
