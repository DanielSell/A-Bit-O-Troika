## Tallow Street: Engendered Intellect Mansions (1)  
Nettie Pomponius    Astrology    3/16/4/0    I3 (1)    Barge Pilot  
  
Captain Pomponius's golden barge was thought lost in a phlogmatic riptide, but a few months ago it quietly docked at the Vicount Mooring Tower with her as the only remaining crew member. No questions were asked -- stranger things happen at sea -- and the captain liquidated her barge and her business interests to purchase an entire block of flats in which to live. This period building has been modified to have the intercom in the style of a golden barge, with each room and the front door connected to a speaking tube, and for each room to be remodelled to connect to the others in a strict sequential way, as in a labyrinth. Captain Pomponius supplements her riches by tutoring wealthy scions in the ways of barge piloting, with each successive tier of instructions and relinquishing of her mysteries allowing deeper entry into the tunnels of her home, but always instructed via tube. None have yet met her in person, but any graduate must inevitably progress towards her.  
  
## Tallow Street: Identical Wisdom House (2)  
Gilded Itch    Hurricane (5)    8/13/2/0    I3 (2)    wax seller, maker of wax images  
Tangled Radiance    Poison    9/8/4/0    I3 (2)    Grocer  
Isaac Theresa    Read Stars (1)    9/18/3/2    I3 (2)    perfumer/druggist  
  
A block of flats built in the primitivist style popular half a century ago, with prominent pink efflorescence and late-era bronze buttresses. The Historic Troika Society have listed the building as of public interest, so the residents are unable to modify the building in any way. Not only can they not fix its peeling walls and rotting floors, they are required to submit house waste, especially dust, for inspection before disposal. As such, most residents have a room reserved for refuse rather than fill out the paper work required, hoping to leave before it becomes a problem. The rent is, however, very reasonable and there are always flats available.
