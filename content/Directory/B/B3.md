## Ephor Way: The Moral Nutshell (1)  
Caulker of barges  
Evaporated Talon Maude    5/17/3/0  
sneak        B3 (1)      
  
THe roof of this drydock is a monumental network of green metal beams and wires on which a small golden barge could alight. Maude is a nimble but black-sun-baked old hand, zipping up the demon-bone pylons on chrism-lubricated pulleys to re-annoint and seal the hull of the barges and remove any stuck on qlippot.  
  
## Demilune Way and Ephor Road: Gammer Gammer Gammer (2)  
Knitter of wool  
Helenus Cage 4/14/4/0    undo        B3 (2)  
  
Institutions from all over Troika send Gammer Gammer Gammer mathmalogical problems to be processed by their team of knitters. At any one time there may be ten or twenty crumblies working under the close supervision of Helenus, who wanders along the line ensuring the accuracy of the pattern. Once they're done the fabrics, some many hundreds of yards long, are baled and sent to the mathmologists who pour over the knit and argue over the findings.  
  
## Ephor Way: Needful Taste of Home (3)  
Druggist  
Tilbert Green    5/15/2/2    Sneak    B2 (3)          
  
A barred window opens on to the street, behind which the well armoured Tilbert Green stands ready to take your order, day or night. He specialises in cultural and religious medicines which are otherwise difficult to obtain for the casual traveller, though tourists are not his only customer. Many locals have developed tastes for some of the experiences on offer, divorced of their traditional application.  
  
## Behind Hypotherm Circus: The Old Bartisan Troupe   
Theatre  
Tufted Gutter    5/12/1/2      
Swim    B2 (4)          
  
A converted torpedo silo turned theatre, home of The Old Bartisan Troupe led by the world famous tumbler Tufted Gutter. Gutter has such immense celebrity that when in public he always wears a breastplate of golden alloy in case of a encounters with a crushing throng or jealous lover. The theatre performs classic plays from the pre-war period, thunderously loud and vertical as they are, so the silo makes a perfect location. On warm nights they even open up the old launch door so the public can watch and jeer from above for free.
