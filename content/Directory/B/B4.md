## Hypotherm Circus: Safe Hands Depository  
Security Master Rembert Zelotes    Ember (2)    4/15/2/3    B4 (2)    safe maker  
Jacques Cinnamon    Sentry    4/13/4/0    B4 (4)    Money changer  
  
Deposit your goods, no matter the size, value or volatility, the staff at the Safe Hands Depository can take care of it. The building is tall and just as deep, full of sealed rooms pre-paid for decades and protected against prying eyes both physical and otherwise. Jacques Cinnamon maintains a dossier of incriminating material on his staff, the severoty of which has never been surpassed by enterprising blackmailers seeking a way in. Security Master Zelotes, who despises the inveigling manager, is one such staff memeber. A mercenary captain of ill-repute who commited countless vile acts in the name of the city in his time, and yet still Cinnamon holds something worse that controls him.
