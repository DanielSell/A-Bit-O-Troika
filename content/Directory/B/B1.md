# B1  
## Apport Mews: Residence of the Bishop of Barley (1)  
 Bishop of Barley 9/16/3/0  
Spells: Natter, Cockroach, Fear, Read Entrails  
  
Bishop is young, willowy, plainly dressed. His home is a multi story townhouse in which he lives alone and does not take guests. The inside is painted a uniform white, every surface undifferentiated and perfectly clean. The only furniture or decoration is a stool on the highest floor positioned between a window overlooking Ephor Way and a freestanding full length silver mirror.  
  
## Apport Mews: Pulk's Lodge (2)  
Hunting lodge  
The Well-Regarded Huntsman-Hermit Cleer Pulk (6/20/3/1)  
Skill: Shoot, Sneak, Track Spell: Ironhand  
Taintstaf the Whippers-In (4/18/2/1)  
Awareness  
Marcoul the Terrier Man (5/10/2/0)  
Run  
  
  
  
The off-season home of Cleer Pulk and his assistants. Polite visitors are received by Marcoul, a saturnine, brutally scarred man who is as broad as they are tall, and provided a tour of the lodges impressive collection of stuffed trophies and weapons. 1 in 3 chance any creature you are looking for is on display. Those looking for help or advice from Pulk are unlikely to find it even if he is in residence (1 in 6), as while he is here he stays in a deep, dry well at the centre of the lodge so as to better plan his next trip. His staff drop charts, books and food down every day at his unspoken request. While his staff are stoic in their refusal to disturb him admirers frequently bribe the baker (18 Blanket Road) to bake letters and invitations into the lodges bread deliveries in the hope of them making their way into Pulk's daily meal.  
  
  
  
## Scones's Way: Bent Keyworks (3)  
Once an arms manufactury, now a burnt out wreck of timber, mangled blades and melted anvils. The local children have taken any salvagable blades.  
  
## Tallow Street: The Blanch Pit (4)  
Now little more than a crater, this fabric bleaching workshop exploded during the fire which tore through the area not long ago leaving nothing but foul smelling rubble.  
  
  

# C1  
## Tallow Street: Leadeater's Gold Pound (1)  
A burnt out gold beater's workshop, now occupied by a gang of gamins who decorate their teeth with the shreds of gold left behind in the ashes and war relentlessly with the yellow scarves and gloves.  
  
## Tallow Street: Cockshut's (2)  
This shop, once selling imported eartenware, is recently abandoned. Through the shttered and boarded window one can spy the probable cause, whereby shelves and shelves of goods appear to have shattered in place and sit there still, left in disgust.   
  
## Tallow Street: Once More, Swine (3)  
The old tanners was relocated out of the neighbourhood after the fire. The building is now inconsistently occupied by children who alternate smashing things and inexpertly tanning hard leather scraps to stuff under their clothes.  
  
## Tallow Street: The Once and Future Spring (4)  
This matress maker took the fire as a sign to retire and shut up shop. The small building is empty and clean, with only the sign to suggest its past.  
  
## Tallow Street: The Fortunate Everyday (5)  
This general good store has been open and unmanned for as long as anyone can remember. The sundry items are always fresh and restocked and customers juditiously place the correct money for their purches behind the counter in an ever growing pile. No one has had the nerve to steal anything, it hasn't even crossed their minds.
