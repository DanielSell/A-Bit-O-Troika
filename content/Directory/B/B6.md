Putti's Buns  
Myste Road #1  
Residents: Fridolin Salvius   (Read Entrails)  9/14/3/0 
A venerable baker's famous for its devotional currant buns. Each is toasted with the Putti, a bisected circular branding iron of contested provenance, and spread inside with butter and thick black jam. Pilgrims used to buy armfuls of them on their way to the Tower of Sabaoth to be hurled at the walls, jam side out, as high and as permanently as possible. Some pilgrims, unaware of the state of the Tower, still brave the daily queue, which regularly reaches all the way to the Box Market during lunch hours. In stark contrast to the jolly baking staff, Fridolin, keeper of the Putti, is an eternally singed, dour man who takes his duties seriously while quietly hoping to be the last to uphold them.
  
## Between Hypotherm Circus and Blanket Road: The Tumbling Rosmarine  
Pia Prima    read stars    4/4/1/0    B6 (2)    Plasterer  
  
From a distance Pia could be mistaken for an active child, but once close it's clear she is in fact quite elderly. She retains a team of scaffolders but otherwise works alone, mixing and ferrying plaster up and down ladders, moulding quarters and covings and the like. Her affordable decorative plasterwork graces many of the homes of Ripplegate and can be identified by its prominent sea mammal themes.  
  
## Blanket Road: Fanwurzel by Tyrannio  
Harrison Tyrannio    Read Stars (1)    5/14/2/0    B6 (3)    Fan Makers  
  
Tyrannio's painted fans are a staple item for those wishing to be seen on their leisurly bathday walks. Each is decorated with a colourful face which is all at once human, demonic, and unplacably cute. They vary in cost from the colourfully human, around 20p, to the disturbingly sinister upwards of 100p. Tryannio claims to only paint what he sees.
