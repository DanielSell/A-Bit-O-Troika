## Hypotherm Circus: Court of the Summer King (1)  
Temple  
Open, the Summer King 9/18/5/0  
 Spell: Assume Shape, Hurricane, Teleport, Zed  
  
 Reception hall for the divine Summer King. Front door and halls guarded by Feathered Folk wielding brass blowpipes-staves decorated with and firing peridots. Petitioners are mostly foreigners and cacogen, with the occasional hyrodules and their silver suited guards. None speak to the King, only touch his hands, drop a guilder in the chrome vase, and leave. The petitioners spend a long time talking in meaningless metaphors to one another. Non-comprehension will be met with disdain.   
  
RUMOUR: The feathered-swine have captured radiant thought and hold it in disgust  
  
## Hypotherm Circus: The Reprimanded Mare  
spur maker  
Macrina Nun    Poison (1)    4/13/2/2    B5 (2)      
Perched like a toad over her work bench Macrina files diligently at the spurs that will go on to cover every surface of the workshop. All sizes, all sharpenesses, for all hides and animals, decorative, functional, brutal and kind. Any kind of spure you could want, she can make them.  
  
## Hypotherm Circus: The Worshipful Company of Tylers  
Tylers Guild Hall  
Tetta Glory    6/14/2/2    True Seeing (3)    B5 (3)          
  
The guild house for the Worshipful Company of Tylers is multi tiered, with cascading roofs each tiled in elaborately cut and flowing tiles befitting the home of the trade. The roofs go low to the ground, showing barely any wall or window, and the inside is plain and mostly empty. The rooms store old tiles and tools along with a small museum exploring the lives of heroic tylers who defined neighbourhoods with they bombastic styles or fearlessly resisted the roftop enclaves of the post-war period. Tetta Glory maintains an office and small staff on the top floor, all tylers of small skill, who spend their time advocating for the rights of tylers everywhere.   
  
## Behind Hypotherm Circus and Stele Mews: Tacceus' Cage  
Meat processor  
Tacceus    7/2/2/1    Starry Orb (4)    B5 (4)          
  
Little more than a roof, a chopping block and a cage. Tacceus is a squat, scalled and hairy man covered in the dried blood of his trade. His independently swiveling eyes have never shed a tear, his heart not skipped a beat. He takes no joy in his job but has a grim pride in the fact he will perform his duty and butcher and prepare any living thing found in the cage of a morning, regardless of its pleas. By ancient bylaw he is well within his rights, which is sometimes taken advantage of by neighbourhood gangs.
