## Apport Mews: The Sun As Seen From Below A Tree (1)  
Draper  
Monstrous Forty    Torpor    9/13/1/0    B2 (1)    Draper  
Calvin Amatorsus    Posthumous Vitality (5)    5/8/2/2    B2 (1)    draper  
  
Before sighting the shop one can tell if it's open by the sound of furious shouting. Forty, the size and shape of a boulder, grabs at the nimble Calvin with hands like pumpkins, while Calvin darts about and slashes at the thick paws with his scissors. They argue about money, they argue about the war, they argue about work and measurements and the weather. Between them they make outstanding curtains to any specification, and the price is very reasonable due to their difficulty with attracting customers. No matter how hard or bloody the previous days arguement, they always come back the next day and greet each other with a sunny "hello".  
  
## Ephor Way: The Agency of Change (2)  
Bank  
Inferior Past 4/3/2/3  
Ember (2)        A9 (2)    money changer  
A small branch of the Spider Bank with a single window run by a quill-specked spider named Inferior Past. The huge building is taken up by a tangle of silk-roped lanes down which customers move toward one of the eight windows at Past's single octagonal booth at the centre. Each in turn ring the bell and the spider spins to serve them as it can, snatching money with delicate sticky feet and counting out coins of exotic sorts from bags retrieved from hidden compartments below her feet. It is, as with all Spider Bank branches, linked to the central record and all wealth can be withdrawn or deposited in complete confidence of its accuracy and safety.  
  
## Ephor Way: Cinder House (3)  
Residence  
Pearl Quietness    3/3/1/3(shell)  
Swim        A9 (3)    cacogen  
  
Well know by locals for its kinetic brass sculptures on its outside walls, bunches of fluted brass funnels like daffodils rotate on their own schedule. Inside this otherwise empty house sits an iron water tank and every fluted funnel leads along a nest of tubes to it, and itself is covered in crooked brass trumpets. Inside the tank floats the ageless cacogen Peary Quietness, who now rarely leaves home or even her tank. Instead she sits and listens to the goings on of the neighbourhood, gathering secrets, beaming brightly with her accumilated and never shared gossip. When she does go abroad with the help of her velare she goes as a damp vagrant with tall bushy hair and wild wandering eyes. She knows fragments of everything, the full picture of nothing, and will never share.
  
## Ephor Way: The Brighter Light (4)  
Burnisher of arms  
Cyrion Senach 6/6/3/0      
Climb        A9 (4)    burnisher of arms  
  
Cyrion's natural position is crouched as though to pounce, ready to grapple whatever surface with his strong limbs and pockets full of cloths and polishes. The workshop is filled with statues, vehicles, cutlery and armour in various states of polishing, which Cyrion is doing at all times. Complusively he will polish whatever is nearby, and when left to his work he will clamber over the pile of work like a squirrel getting in to every nook with his abrasives. And, like a squirrel, he often gets distracted and forgets where he leaves his rags.
