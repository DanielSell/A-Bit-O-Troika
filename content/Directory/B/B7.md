# B7  
  
## The Brown Labyrinth: The door quadrant (1)  
Approaching from the gate (B7 1) one sees four doors block the four entrances to the labyrinth here. On each door is a plaster face of a goblin which, when approached, wakes up along with its three friends. They demand a toll to enter, a tax for the goblin king, to be deposited in their mouths to the sum of 5p per person to pass through them. The north-west claims they lead directly to the goblin hoard and should be avoided, the north-east says they lead to the contus crater, the south west claims they lead to the goblin hoard where your riches can be made, and the south east refuses to divulge its secrets. Anyone entering the two doors claiming to lead to the hoard will be watched carefully by the goblin guards back at the gate.  
  
Approaching from behind the doors look normal and open easily. There is no sign of the money.  
  
## The Brown Labyrinth: A suspicious shortcut (3)  
  
A worn wooden ladder leads down into a brick well which can be entered into by anything equal to or small than an adult human. The steps creak under weight but they are safe, the air is humid and the faint sound of crashing waves can be heard. After a few minutes of climbing one reaches the bottom in the pitch blackness. Feeling around or bringing a light will reveal a metal door with a simple handle. The door is stuck and requires a feat of strength to open, and once opened leads out into the sunlight at B9 (4).
