# B9  
## Found Text (1)  
A particularly large slab in the wall has weather worn text carved into it. Much of it is cut away from when it was fitted snugly into the wall but what remains reads:  
  
"*..... stately pride deface;*  
*.... plays harmless round the marble base,*  
*In vain the Tempest, and in vain the blast,*  
*The Trident is confirmed:.....*"  
  
And at the end, in fresh pink paint, is scrawled with a flourish: "*the dream is past!*"  
  
  
  
## The public work (2)  
Leobald    Levitate (2)    4/14/2/0    B9 (4)    brass worker  
The goblin Leobald is shaping a brass panel attached to a door in the wall. He hammers it with a huge hammer and steps back to admire his work. He explains that it is a portrait of the Goblin King, in progress. Any attempts to open the door will be prevented with shouts for guards and blows of the hammer. Roll for a random labyrinth encounter. The door is purely decorative, and does not open. If forced, one finds a bare wall behind it.
