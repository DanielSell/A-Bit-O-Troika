# C9  
  
## The Brassy Hoard  
CHICKEN LITTE FROM SPACE MERCHANTS  
Parisius Mancius    Hurricane (5)    4/11/4/0    C9 (1)    Tallow Chandlers (tallow candle makers)  
Defender of the Brassy Hoard Lock (1) 11/18/4/4  
  
Three small doors sit slightly above ground level, one on each wall of the building. Outside each door stands a goblin, each sealed up in metal armour, each differently cast in wild faces, plumes, frills, spikes and manes. They don't move or talk unless someone approaches with the intent to enter the hoard (a thought they can sense a mile away), at which they will move with un-Goblin-like efficiency to dispatch the burglar. The courtyard surrounding the hoard is kept diligently clean by some unseen custodian, who neatly avoids a generous circle around where the guards stand, leaving a dusty, blood stained circle at each entrance. The doors are sealed by magic means, and must be torn down or disenchanted to enter, after which the prospective burgular would find a solid wall of coins of every sort, packed tight against the door and corroded together, and require a firm hand and plenty of time to dislodge. Parisius Mancius, found sat upon a pile of old books from which he reads, operates a small ortolan stand in the western corner of the courtyard serving the infrequent pilgrims and tourists that visit the site. In exchange for all present buying and eating a poached ortolan (66p each and served in a torn out page of a book) he cheerfully tells you everything he knows about this place, including the fact that the guards are not on good terms, and will not assist the others when trouble approaches, each considering their duty to be their door alone.
