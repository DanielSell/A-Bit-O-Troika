## Hypotherm Circus: St Cuthman the Unwilling's Church  
Eviscerated Youth    Ember    3/18/4/0    C4 (2)  
  
Through the glass front of the church one can see bins of brightly coloured boiled sweets, thousands upon thousands of them. Much of the congregations regular worhsip is the communal manufacture of these confections under the guidance of Father Youth. The sweets are cheap and delicious, except for one in every hundred which possesses a taste so foul as to ruin your week. Usually it is a simple unpleasant flavour, but occasionally the church gets some exotic ingredients and makes sweets which are hot, induce mild seizures, cause excessive drolling, or so on. Harmless fun. The sweets are wildly popular with local children, who pool their money and take turns to see who gets St Cuthman's sweet.  
  
## Hypotherm Circus: Tower of Sabaoth (2)  
Magnus Magnus    Protection from Rain (1)    3/17/1/0    C4 (1)    soap-maker  
Aunt Peter    Sneak    7/16/2/0    C4 (1)    knight  
  
The ancient silo tube, red and rotten with rust, houses a rare shrine to all-saints. It is, as per tradition, without a priest of any sort, instead it is maintained by the custodian Magnus Magnus, and Aunt Peter, a knight of all-saints. Magnus is old, bent double and crusty like an armadillo, but is quick with his knife and a font of local knowledge for paying visitors. Aunt Peter is an indomitable tower of steel, she stands day and night leaning heavily on her massive pike. People suspect she sleeps standing up which would explain the lack of breaks, yet no one has managed to catch her out. The maintenance and wages are paid for out of a combination of the profits of Magnus' while-you-wait carved soap saints and donations gathered from pilgrims and the Friends of Sabaoth community charity.
