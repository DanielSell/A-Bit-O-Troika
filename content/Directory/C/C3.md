## Familiarity Way: The Troikan Mission of Funest  
Noiseless Palmatius    Mirror Selves (3)    5/2/2/1    C3 (1)    mattress/bedding maker  
  
This building was an embassy for the Realms of Haunting until they were expelled for inflammatory remarks in the Chief Alcalde's presence. Now it is a mission for the harbinger of the end times, Funest. The inside remains much as it did while it was an embassy except that a floor has been removed to make space for a blood soaked monolith brought from the last sphere that fell to His legions. Palmatius silently mans the mission, ready to anoint any who enter and swear allegiance to the bringer of the end times. Palmatius does not sleep or eat, being maintained by his sinister patron's insatiable energies, so it is open all hours and welcomes anyone curious in the faith. The locals have not been eager to join, and the only people to be found here are local cab drivers using it as a rest stop during rainy weather. As a result most local public transport drivers have a bloody handprint on their face and are casually committed to bringing ruin to the city on the dark lords arrival.  
  
## Familiarity Way: Great Community Gift Space  
Heavenly Vale    Etiquette    5/10/2/0    C3 (2)    warehouse keeper  
Mildred Ursus    8/12/1/3    Zed (?)    C3 (2)        furrier  
  
This warehouse space is available to all merchants of Ripplegate through the benevolence of the Universal Congress, on registration with Alcalde Station Juniper and waiving of certain rights. All hours of the day and night it is open and guarded by ex-soldiers Mildred and Vale. They take turns sitting on a stool outside with the company fusil and napping. Anyone holding a merchant pass signed by the local chief alcalde may enter and leave whatever they please in a designated stall, all they have to do is list what is there, how much is is worth, and where you got it and post that to the alcalde station no later than 6 days after deposit or withdrawal. Simple.   
  
## Familiarity Way: M. D. Guise (5)  
Fred Guise    Cockroach (5)    5/13/1/1    C3 (3)    Wax Chandlers (wax candle makers)  
  
Fred was once a door to door chandler, and a poor one at that. Until one day he knocked on a door and a desperate thinking engine sought to avoid their fate my commissioning Guise to make wax dolls of him, as many as he could. That way no curse could find him. Fred did as such but when he returned for payment the engine was nowhere to be found, dropped off the face of the city. In an effort to recover some of the lost money and time he started selling the little engine dolls alongside his candles. Demand went wild, children loved them, and now Fred and his family make nothing other than little thinking engine dolls of his missing patron.  
  
## Familiarity Way: Mansion of Fish (4)  
Timber merchant  
Chrysogonus Longus    Amity (4)    7/2/1/1    C3 (4)    sawer of wood  
  
This steel ancient silo towers over the surrounding buildings. The rusted walls keep the rain out for the most part, and where it doesn't Longus puts up tarpaulins to protect his stock. Day and night a small fire burns in the base of the building, drying out timber arrayed awkwardly along its curved interior. Small animals often make the mistake of nesting in the higher shelves only to be desicated and discovered later as perfect sleeping mummies. Longus gives these animals away to the hildren of his customers to distract them while their parent discusses business.   
  
  
## Familiarity Way: Seaview House  
Residential  
Percy Eutychius    7/3/2/1    Earthquake (5)    C3 (5)        tourist  
  
This pale blue plastered manor has been unclaimed for years. The local council maintain it by leasing it to extra-planar visitors on the grounds that they keep it in sound condition and to host a salon one evening a week for locals to come and visit. Percy Eutychius has been here for several months and has been too polite to add to the almost absent furniture, so his salons are standing affairs. He appears mild and rather boring, but it is in fact a cunning tactic to avoid talking about himself and risking revealing his status as a wealthy salt mine owner from a few districts over and not foreign at all.
