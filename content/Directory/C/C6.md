# C6  
## Behind Blanket Road: Warehouse 51  
Wulmar Abakuh    Slide Skywards (6)    5/18/3/2    C6 (1)    Cutlers (knife, sword and utensil makers)  
Wulmar makes only a few pieces per year. Knives, axes, guns, pilum, anything that takes his fancy he'll make and people will line up all the way to the plaza of time for a chance to bid on it. When the call goes up that Wulmar has returned from his "material research" travels and is making a mystery project, the line starts. Day and night people file past putting a slip of paper under the door with their offer and contact detail, they don't care what it is they're buying, it's a Wulmar! His pieces aren't functionally better than a cheap example from the market, but his designs are distinctive and can open social doors for the owner.  
  
## Blanket Road: Meat Maniple  
Isaac Lutgardis    Healing    3/11/2/1    C6 (2)    sausage-maker  
Isaac is a meatpacker, in a long line of meat packers. He looks like the sausages he makes his living from, thick, mottled, and fit to bursting with flavour. His bright red face is always frowning now the goblin labyrinth has started to ruin his business with their cheap mystery meat. All anyone wants is goblin meat cutlets, nobody wants his lovingly packed meat any more.   
  
## Blanket Road: Contra Naturam  
Hildegard Dung    Flash (3)    6/12/4/0    C6 (3)    Glaziers and Painters of Glass  
  
A glass workshop by this name has been on the spot for almost a hundred years and has had many owners, each of which has come to an unpleasant end. Habitual Cassian, who founded the workshop after St. Goran levelled the neighbourhood, was eaten by foxes while locking up for the night; Tragic Absadi managed to fall entirely into his kiln and live for three days; Euplius Crux was poetically poured over with molten glass for his monarchist sympathies; Aldemar Crux suffocated after getting his head stuck in a water pitcher; Triangle Lovit melted after sealing himself in a barrel of acid; Brynoth tripped and impaled himself on his blowpipe; and Hildegard Dung is still standing. For now.
