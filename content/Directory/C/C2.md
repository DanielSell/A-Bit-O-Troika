# C2  
## Tallow Street: Hounds of Abuse (1)  
Hatter  
Lazarus Stop-Zoticus        5/8/4/1    C2 (1)    Feltmakers (hat makers)  
Torpor (3)  
  
Thanks to the firey pamphlets given away with every order, this hat shop has become a hotspot of monarchist sentiment. The street outside is covered in graffiti, "Unequal minds!", "May the king live forever", "I didn't vote for it", and so on in typical fashion. Lazarus' headwear has become associated with the movement so closely that just wearing one of his hats can provoke the alcaldes to follow you all afternoon.  
  
## Tallow Street: The Signing Sound Butchers (2)  
Butcher of fowl  
Strapping Eugenia        8/8/3/1    C2 (2)  
Ward (1)  
  
Strapping Eugenis stands in the window counter of her stall nonchalantly hewing off heads and removing bones from birds of all sorts while maintaining a constant chatter with the public walking by whether they like it or not. In quiet moments she directs her banter towards the rebellious youths hanging around outside the hat shop across the street while cutting delicate crowns of gizzards.  
  
## Between Tallow Street and Ephor Way: The Windery (3)  
Rope twister  
Marculf Bassa-Pierino 6/15/2/0      
Fire bolt        C2 (3)    twister of rope  
  
Marcoulf sits on a stool in the centre of the workshop, his eyes focus only on his hands, and he talks in a distracted whisper. Ropes pile up in the shop with no clear path in or out, and the smells of the street are masked by the overwhelming musk of warm jute. Through holes in the ceiling threads descend which he gently pulls and winds to make all manner of cord. The thread from above never stops, and so neither does Marculf.  
  
## Tallow Street: The Door of Horn  
Mattress maker  
Innocent Pit 3/4/2/1  
Peace (4)        C2 (1)    mattress/bedding maker  
  
Pit holds onto himself like he might otherwise fall apart. His mattresses were the finest in town, and still are in many ways, however customers keep coming back with complaints of live animals crawling amongst the box springs on the first night home. At his wits end, the shop is closed, but Pit remains inside tearing apart and reassembling his beds to figure out how it happens.
