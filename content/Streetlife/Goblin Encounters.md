## The Brown Labyrinth: A confounding junction (1)  
This four way junction is cunningly made with the finest goblin mechanical illusions so that, while it looks plain and simple, it is in fact full of blinds and perspectives at odd angles. This has the entertaining (to goblin minds) effect of causing the people entering the junction to leave at random directions unless they test their luck. People leaving by different directions seem to dissapear from each others view as if by magic.  
  
## The Brown Labyrinth: Falling flagstones (2)  
Hiding under the flagstones of this junction are a team of diminutive subterranenan goblins. It is the height of hilarity for them to remove the stone beneath a characters feet and sink them to their crotch in the ground. They may test their luck to avoid this, otherwise they sink and take damamge as unarmed from the fall. Laughter is heard frum underneath and the stones are replaced once the party leaves.
