# Appendix  
  
## Appendix A: The Brown Labyrinth  
Climbind up and over the walls of the labyrinth is impossible. Anyone doing so will find themselves in a random section of the labyrinth with no idea to where they are. They are lost. Those walking along the walls will just see unremarkable corridors forever, unending, until they descend and submit to the logic of the goblins.  
  
At the western edge: The Great Waste  
Ahead a dry wind blows, smelling like salt and sulfer. It leads to the Great Waste, where feral goblin hordes roam and gather in preparation for their next assault against their labyrinth dwelling kin.   
  
At the southern edge: Forbidden Road to the Goblin City  
An endless passage. Any attempt to return fails, instead going straight forever with no turns in sight. Every day, if they walk all day, there is a 1 in 6 chance that the party happens upon a damaged wall with a hole leading to a random off-map entry to the brown labyrinth. Every day each of them lose one ration or d6 stamina. Anyone trying to climb out will return to where they started. The only way to pass through here is to have a goblin guide willing to takw you to the city, which is unlikely. Any goblin saying they'll take you through has a 111 in d666 chance of not immediately abandoning you once they have the chance.  
  
Encounters:  
  
11: The goblin anthropologist  
Alban Twine    Sleep    7/16/3/0    C7 (1)    scribe  
Alban Twine the goblin anthropologist targets you as the topic for his latest paper, *Invasive Species From the Pomerium*. He is dressed in old pieces of carpet, glued or stitched together like an upholstered ghillie suit. He scratches illegible notes in a pig skin journal while he follows you not far behind, so close you can clearly smell his boiled turnip aroma. He has a strict non-intervention policy and will not help or acknowledge the party no mater how much trouble they get in. If attacked he will deploy an alarmingly large Fusil he had strapped under his clothes for just such an eventuality. 1 in 6 chance every day that he finishes his work and returns to the city to publish his paper.  
  
22: The Bottle  
On a specially made, decorative stool stands a corked fiasco bottle and tin cup. The glass is dark but there is clearly liquid inside. The liquid is treacly and smells strongly of wet dog. This liquid is special reserve owl wine, left here and elsewhere around the labyrinth by Royal Wine Maintenance teams to revitalise tired (goblin) travelers. If this is consumed neat by a non-goblin they must test their luck or temporarily lose their sight and permanently lose the ability to whistle. Blindness lasts until they sleep it off for 8 hours. If the owl wine is diluted by at least 2:1 (or drunk by a goblin at any strength) it counts as a ration. The bottle holds 12 stiff drinks, and anyone caught stealing or carrying these very distinct bottles will be in trouble from any goblins who spot them.  
  
33: Lobbed Rubbish  
The party hears a distant "*hyup!* followed by a burlap sack of rubbish flying over the labyrinth wall. They may test their luck to avoid being hit (as club) by the cascading crap. Appears to be a picnic, utensils and all, stuffed into the bag. There are enough leftovers for two rations worth of food if eaten there and then, out of the bin, like an urban fox.  
  
44: Drunk Goblins  
Three goblins stagger along holding each other up. They are hopped up on owl wine, half blind, and ready to cause trouble. They insist that the party are in their kitchen, that they have half a mind to give them a jolly good stabbing, and that they will only refrain from persecuting them if they make them something to eat since they have unexpectedly lost the use of most of their fine motor skills and are very hungry. Give them three rations or else fight them, they are armed with small Swords
