In Troika, child rearing is of a sort. Children are expected to entertain themselves between Lauds and Vespers, and entertain themselves they do. On the streets one finds a juvenile microcosm: armies march, kings and queens rise and fall, lives are spent, and fortunes are earned and lost, and at the age of thirteen, it all ends as children enter the higher world of adulthood. Adults have short memories, and the dramas now unfolding look rather like gangs of armed children blocking alleys and stealing cakes, and nothing like the mythological childhood of their own formative years.

Special:
Slingshots do damage as Club but at range. Gamins encountered north of Box Market and west of Ripple Road will likely (1 in 2) have a supply (2d6) of the bright yellow bullets they call "pisspuffs" which explode in a cloud of caustic yellow dust when they hit you, dealing damage as Club, blinding you for d6 hours unless you are Lucky, and permanently staining anything it touches a shade of yellow which elicits shouts of "piss pants piss pants" from every gamin you meet in the street. It washes off skin in a month.
