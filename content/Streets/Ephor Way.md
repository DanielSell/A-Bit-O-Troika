

Named after its historic use as the final road taken by local magistrates on their way to present grievances at the praetorium that once stood on Hypotherm Circus.

A1 - Buildings have been burnt out and fire damaged. Traffic is blocked by a mountain of spilt iron slag from The Ironwerk. During the day several council road workers take turns being outside explaining to angry locals that they're waiting on appropriate tools and hiding in their red and white stripped construction tent playing cards until it's time to go home.

A2 & B2 & B3 - A noisy, busy street. Its two banks and the kinetic sculpture of Cinder House inevitably attract whatever tourists the borough harbours. Wealthier tourists sometimes enter the borough through the Golden Barge mast on top of The Moral Nutshell. When a barge is present the street will throng with tourists and locals alike here to watch the spectacle.
